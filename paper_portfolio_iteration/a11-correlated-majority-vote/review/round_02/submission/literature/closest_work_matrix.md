## Positioning boundary

The nearest work supports a careful, narrow novelty statement: this manuscript
studies a posterior certificate for a *specified count-exchangeable replay*
endpoint. It does not establish a first online early stopper, nor compare
against every adaptive test-time scaling system under a common online
correctness-and-cost protocol. Such a claim remains blocked on
`EXP-ORDERED-CORRECTNESS-COST`.
