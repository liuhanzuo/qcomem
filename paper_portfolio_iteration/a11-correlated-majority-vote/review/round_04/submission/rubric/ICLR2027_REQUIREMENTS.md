## Submission gates used in this workspace

- Abstract deadline: 2026-09-18 AOE.
- Full-paper deadline: 2026-09-25 AOE.
- Initial-submission main text: at most 9 pages. References and appendices are
  excluded; reviewers are not required to read appendices.
- Double blind: author identity must not appear in the paper or supplement.
- Mandatory AI-use statement; it does not count toward the page limit.
- Reproducibility statement is strongly recommended and does not count toward
  the main-text limit.
- Use the official ICLR 2027 style files.
