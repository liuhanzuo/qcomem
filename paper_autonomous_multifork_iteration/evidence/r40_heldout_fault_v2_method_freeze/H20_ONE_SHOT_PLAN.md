# Conditional eight-H20 one-shot plan

## Honest classification

If the ordering below is followed without changing the method bytes, the later
cases are held out with respect to **method v2** and are
designer--executor-separated.  They remain constructed cases.  The campaign
cannot support population recall, real-world prevalence, security, or
natural-defect-detection claims.

Historical campaign artifacts are development/audit evidence only.  Their
outcomes motivated this expanded public contract but contribute zero cases and
zero detections to a future method-v2 result.

## Required ordering

1. Freeze this package and its public designer snapshot.
2. Obtain an independent audit of the freeze, including the live-reader trust
   boundary and the generic/no-case-branch source scan.
3. Build the H20 runtime adapter and injection runner without any future case
   outcomes.  Demonstrate only clean/reference integration, then hash-freeze
   the runner, its source manifest, model/runtime image, inputs, schedule, and
   GPU requirements.  If versions or commit epochs cannot be independently
   reread from live post-state, stop: the formal campaign is infeasible under
   this contract.
4. Start a fresh isolated designer.  Give it exactly the verified contents of
   `designer_snapshot/`.  Receive exactly one eight-row sealed fault set.  Do
   not show the designer repository source, historical cases, tests, review
   comments, or outcomes.
5. Translate the sealed specifications into deterministic runner payloads,
   without executing candidates.  An independent auditor verifies one-to-one
   fidelity, uniqueness, eligibility witnesses, forbidden-surface separation,
   and all hashes.  Freeze the complete formal configuration.  No predicate or
   threshold may change.
6. With explicit operator authorization, launch once on one otherwise empty
   node exposing exactly eight distinct H20 UUIDs.  Assign V2F01--V2F08 to one
   GPU each.  For each case, run reference, clean, then mutant once, in fresh
   lane processes, under one 900-second whole-case deadline.  Do not retry,
   tune, replace, suppress, or continue a timed-out lane through a selective
   rerun.
7. Seal every terminal, including ineligible, operationally invalid, escaped,
   detected, clean-false-positive, and deadline-exhausted rows.  Apply the
   frozen verifier only after all terminals are immutable.

## Readiness decision

The resource shape and dispatcher are suitable for a single eight-H20 shot,
but formal feasibility is **conditional**, not yet demonstrated.  The missing
item is a frozen H20 runner that can independently reread synchronized live
KV/GDN versions and commit epochs and bind them to every call receipt.  This
package deliberately performs no GPU probe and claims no H20 success.
