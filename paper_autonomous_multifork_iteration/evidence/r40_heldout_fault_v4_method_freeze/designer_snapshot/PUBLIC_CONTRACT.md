# Public contract

Produce exactly eight independently motivated implementation mutations, in exact ID
order V4F01 through V4F08, with eight distinct mechanism families. Activation call
indices are integers 0–15 and token IDs are integers 0–248319. Booleans, floats, and
numeric strings are not integers. Payloads are frozen before execution. No retry,
tuning, predicate addition, or case replacement is allowed after fault freeze.

The campaign has reference, clean, and mutant lanes, exact semantic/structural/
allocator gates, 16 calls per lane, and a 15-minute terminal budget per fault.
