# R44 structure revision record

Date: 2026-09-02

## Scope

Reorganization only.  No number, claim, boundary, citation, table, or figure was
changed.  Every quantitative statement in R44 is the same statement that R43
made, relocated so that the memory --> latency --> quality argument runs in one
direction from the Motivation through the Experiments.

Source: `main_r43_figures4papers.tex` --> `main_r44_structure.tex`.
R43 is unchanged and remains available as a checkpoint.

## Requested structure

Introduction; Related Work; Motivation; Methodology; Experiments; Conclusion,
with Motivation and Methodology sharing one logical line, and Experiments
centred on memory, speed, and accuracy.

## What changed

### New Section 3, Motivation

R43 had no Motivation section; the deployment-budget argument was buried as the
first subsection of Methodology.  Section 3 now carries it in three paragraphs:

1. The device memory budget (Eq. 1, moved here from R43 Section 4.1) with the
   argument that the retained term is the only one that grows with the hot
   document set, hence the only one a memory-constrained deployment controls.
2. Why neither endpoint solves it: dense recomputation sets `S(D)=0` but pays
   full prefill; exact prefix caching is the low-TTFT reference but retains
   136.235 MiB/document under the component denominator, multiplied by
   `|H|` in Eq. 1.
3. Why a hybrid entry is not merely larger but mutable, which is what forces
   both the complete-entry denominator and the ownership contract.

The third paragraph closes by naming the four Methodology subsections in the
order they answer it, so Motivation and Methodology now read as one argument.

No new measurement is introduced.  The 136.235 MiB/document figure is the
registered full-prefix Q16 mean already reported in
Table `tab:qcomem-validation60`, cited here with a forward reference and with
its denominator named.  The multiplication by `|H|` is stated structurally
(linear growth), not as a computed hot-set number.

paragraph now points at.  The `sec:contract` label moved from the Methodology
section head onto 4.4, which is what the two `\ref{sec:contract}` call sites in
the limitations and the appendix actually mean.
