## A2 — the missing paired interval exists, and it supports the paper

All ten pairwise paired item-level bootstrap intervals, 10,000 resamples, seed
20260903, computed over the 60 common item keys:

| pair | mean diff | 95% CI |
|---|---:|---|
| dense-prefill-once − full-prefix-q16 | -0.4881 | [-4.5660, 2.5509] |
| dense-prefill-once − full-prefix-q8 | 3.2949 | [-2.4786, 9.4520] |
| dense-prefill-once − qcomem-d13 | 2.9071 | [-2.8268, 8.9943] |
| dense-prefill-once − qcomem-d7 | -0.1173 | [-4.4576, 3.1106] |
| full-prefix-q16 − full-prefix-q8 | 3.7831 | [-0.1914, 9.3195] |
| full-prefix-q16 − qcomem-d13 | 3.3952 | [-0.5737, 8.7639] |
| full-prefix-q16 − qcomem-d7 | 0.3708 | [-1.0533, 2.0345] |
| full-prefix-q8 − qcomem-d13 | -0.3878 | [-5.3363, 4.6122] |
| **full-prefix-q8 − qcomem-d7** | **-3.4122** | **[-8.4296, -0.0390]** |
| qcomem-d13 − qcomem-d7 | -3.0244 | [-8.2344, 0.5789] |

The bold row is the one the panel said was missing and that R46 and R47 could
only report as a difference of means. **Its interval excludes zero**, so on the
paired test the quality advantage of frozen depth-7 over the quantized exact
cache is supported at the 95 percent level, not merely observed. The manuscript
sentence stating that no such interval was computed and that the retained-state
ordering is therefore the firmer of the two is now obsolete and understates what
the evidence supports.

Two supporting readings. Frozen depth-7 against full-prefix Q16 is
0.3708 [-1.0533, 2.0345], which crosses zero, so the method remains
indistinguishable from the unquantized exact cache. Dense against frozen
depth-7 is -0.1173 [-4.4576, 3.1106], also crossing zero.

Note the upper bound is -0.0390, which is close to zero. The honest statement is
that the interval excludes zero, not that the effect is large or robust; a
different seed could plausibly move an endpoint across it. Report the seed and
the resampling unit alongside the interval, as the paper already does elsewhere.

## A8 and C4 — the latency panel does not compose because dispersion is enormous

Per-repeat spread over the three declared repeats, and the median generated-token
count actually produced under `--eos-policy stop`:

| config | TTFT min / med / max (s) | TPOT min / med / max (ms) | median generated tokens |
|---|---|---|---:|
| dense-prefill-once | 0.2587 / 0.6482 / 1.4729 | 50.60 / 53.46 / 86.63 | 4 |
| full-prefix-q16 | 0.1500 / 0.1610 / 1.0891 | 50.53 / 53.45 / **404.01** | 4 |
| full-prefix-q8 | 0.1545 / 0.1646 / 1.3874 | 50.73 / 53.46 / 89.93 | 5 |
| qcomem-d13 | 0.3192 / 0.5826 / 1.5740 | 51.51 / 54.18 / 89.34 | 4 |
| qcomem-d7 | 0.3527 / 0.6683 / 1.5296 | 51.56 / 54.19 / 86.84 | 4 |

Reconstructing throughput as `n / (TTFT + (n-1) * TPOT)` and comparing against
the recorded value gives a median relative error of 7.6 to 18.8 percent
depending on the arm. That is the arithmetic failure two PDF-only reviewers and
the meta-reviewer each found independently, and this table is its cause: the
maxima sit two to seven times above the medians, one TPOT maximum is 404 ms
against a 53 ms median, and generation stops after four or five tokens rather
than the 32 the protocol permits.

Reporting means alone conceals all of this. The correction is not a repaired
point estimate but a change of what is reported: give min, median and max per
cell, state the aggregation rule, and state the median generated-token count so
a reader can see the throughput denominator is four tokens rather than the cap.

## A4 — the reconciliation closes, in favour of the earlier explanation

Document token counts are identical across arms in this run, min 1146, median
4000, max 4050, and the full-prefix mean Store is 136.2354 MiB/document,
matching the archival cohort exactly. So the 136.235-versus-140.34 gap is not an
estimand difference between the two panels; 140.34 is the eight-item median. The
earlier decomposition into roughly 69 percent cohort composition and 31 percent
mean-versus-median stands, and the manuscript's "3.8 percent longer documents"
sentence should be replaced by the median-versus-mean explanation rather than
defended.

## Manuscript consequences

1. Replace the no-paired-interval concession with -3.4122 [-8.4296, -0.0390],
   with its seed, and note the upper bound's proximity to zero.
2. Report dispersion for every timing cell and the median generated-token count;
   until that is done the latency panel should not present bare means.
3. Replace the 3.8 percent length explanation with the mean-versus-median one.
4. None of this licenses a latency claim. Frozen depth-7 remains behind both
   exact caching and honest dense recomputation on TTFT.
