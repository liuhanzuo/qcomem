Build: `build/r51_bodytable_v1/`. Date: 2026-09-03.

## 1. The defect this round fixes

R50 made total memory the paper's central claim — `M_total(D,N) = S·D + T(N)` leads the
abstract, an introduction bullet, the Motivation, Section 5.2 and the Conclusion — but both
tables that carry it (`tables/qcomem_composition_r50.tex`, the crossover and worked deployment
points, and `tables/qcomem_transient_r50.tex`, the measured per-fanout transient) sat in
Appendix C as Tables 5 and 4, cited from body pages 8 and 9. The nine-page body contained one
figure and one table, and neither supported the lead claim. A reader of the body could not
check it.

## 6. Page arithmetic — why the transient panel could not also reach the body

Measured on the ICLR 2026 template: 54 lines per page, ~12.1 pt per line. R50's body ends flush
at the bottom of page 9 (last baseline 730 pt against a 734 pt text-block bottom), so the body
had ~0 lines of usable slack.

Four builds were measured:

| Build | Body change vs R50 | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| probe 1 | merged (a)+(b) float added, Table 1 kept | Conclusion pushed to page 10; overflow ≈ 24 lines |
| probe B | crossover float only, Table 1 kept | Conclusion pushed to page 10; overflow ≈ 13 lines |
| probe C | merged (a)+(b) float added, Table 1 moved out | fits, page 9 with ~2 lines spare |
| **final** | crossover float added, Table 1 moved out, panel numbers restated in prose | **fits, page 9 with 0.3 lines spare** |

From probes B and C: the merged two-panel float costs ~24 body lines, the crossover float alone
~13, and the 60-item panel is worth ~26. Restating that panel's ~50 otherwise-unstated numbers
in prose costs ~13 lines. So:

* crossover only, panel kept in body → **13 lines short**;
* merged (a)+(b), panel moved out + prose → 24 − 26 + 13 = **11 lines short**;
* crossover only, panel moved out + prose → 13 − 26 + 13 = **0** ← shipped.

Closing an 11- or 13-line gap would require cutting ~165–195 words of body prose. A pass over
the body found ~60–80 words of genuinely promotional text (framing clauses in the Introduction,
Section 4.2's "Three reimplementation details the equation omits", the run-in sentence opening
Section 5.6 that restates Section 5.1) — about 4–5 lines. Everything beyond that is scope, a
non-claim, a limitation, or an adverse number, all of which the constraints protect. The
remaining slack in the body is 6.3 lines total and 3.5 of those are on page 3, held open by
Figure 1. The merged float is therefore not reachable without breaking a hard constraint, and
the crossover table — the one the instruction names — is in the body instead.
