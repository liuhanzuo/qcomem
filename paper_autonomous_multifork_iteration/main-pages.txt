                                                   A BSTRACT
010
011            Forking many continuations from one long prefix creates an ownership question
012            that output equality alone cannot resolve: branches may agree while aliasing
013            mutable recurrent state, and compared paths may share a defect. We present
014            F ORK AUDIT, a receipt-bound ownership audit, and evaluate it as a fixed sequential
015            case study on one Qwen3.5-35B-A3B configuration. The runtime under test may
016            violate ownership, lifecycle, or call contracts; the capture producer is assumed
017            to faithfully emit every mandatory record and bind referenced bytes to archived
018
               artifacts. Under this assumption, offline replay checks prefix immutability, re-
               quest/base and request/peer disjointness, tail copy-on-write, first-transition GDN
019
               rebinding, recorded Python-level call contracts, and cross-arm/cross-N consis-
020
               tency. It cannot detect omitted or fabricated producer records or independently
021            attest compiled dispatch. Across eight pre-specified books, 96 configurations
022            cross full-copy/shared-document KV with materialized/borrowed GDN bases at
023            N ∈ {1, 8, 32}. Registered token, canonical full-logit, logical-KV, and final-GDN
024            digests match across all cells and 288 adjacent-fan-out comparisons, while phase
025            receipts witness post-transition GDN disjointness. Eight selected attention rows
026            pass a dense-FP32 oracle, four preregistered GDN transitions pass a NumPy recur-
027            rence, and nine designed faults reach their predeclared first gates before producing
028            post-injection semantics. The latter is localization, not a baseline miss or detection-
029
               rate estimate. A same-stack, two-geometry cancellation/reclamation extension
               preserves clean semantics and storage in 16/16 cells and reaches the expected
030
               gate in 48/48 preregistered schedule-fault trials. At N = 32, shared KV reduces
031
               median final allocated delta from 4.90 to 2.23 GiB. The case study establishes
032            feasibility on the specified stack, not independent recapture, concurrent-kernel
033            safety, end-to-end verification, or capacity.

036   1    I NTRODUCTION
037
038   Best-of-N decoding, agents, tree search, and document question answering create many continuations
039   from a common prefix. Modern serving systems exploit this structure through paged KV caches,
040   prefix trees, and scheduling policies (Kwon et al., 2023; Zheng et al., 2024; Gim et al., 2024; Srivatsa
041   et al., 2025). For a Transformer, “sharing the prefix” can often be described as sharing read-only KV
042   blocks and giving each continuation private append blocks. Hybrid models complicate this picture:
043   full-attention layers coexist with recurrent or linear-attention layers whose compact state is updated
044
      in place unless the runtime installs an explicit request-local view (Yang et al., 2025; Lenz et al., 2025;
      Pan et al., 2025).
045
046   This creates two evaluation hazards. First, output equality is only a relational observation: branches
047   can agree while sharing mutable state, and compared implementations can share a defect. An owner-
048   ship audit must additionally witness prefix immutability, request/base and request/peer disjointness,
049   tail copy-on-write, first-transition recurrent rebinding, and the call contract that produced the re-
050
      sult. Second, “memory saving” may refer to logical payload, allocated cache pages, framework
      allocator peaks, process-level device memory, or admitted service capacity. Those quantities are not
051
      interchangeable.
052
053   We propose F ORK AUDIT, a correctness-first receipt contract for this setting. The audited runtime may
      contain implementation faults, but the capture producer is assumed to record mandatory objects and

      Paged and prefix-aware inference. PagedAttention, Prompt Cache, RadixAttention, ChunkAt-
111   tention, Hydragen, and Preble establish block sharing, prefix reuse, shared-prefix computation, and
112   scheduling as prior art (Kwon et al., 2023; Gim et al., 2024; Zheng et al., 2024; Ye et al., 2024;

116   Hybrid state and cache management. Gated Delta Networks maintain mutable compact state
117   unlike token-indexed KV (Yang et al., 2025); Jamba is a hybrid architecture and Marconi manages
118   hybrid-model prefixes (Lenz et al., 2025; Pan et al., 2025). HYPIC caches segment transition
119   operators plus zero-start end states and recomputes a small seam to enable position-independent

      does not replace these policies: it audits their ownership boundary. Appendix Table 12 keeps their

      original-protocol numbers separate from our common-model controls.

147   Threat model and assurance boundary. The audited runtime may contain implementation faults
148   that violate registered ownership, lifecycle, storage-view, or Python-level call contracts. We trust the
149   capture producer to record every mandatory event faithfully and to bind each referenced byte object
150   to an archived artifact. Detached SHA-256 ledgers and schema replay detect missing, inconsistent, or
151   post-capture modified records; they are integrity bindings, not a second live observer. Replay can
152   therefore reject recorded aliasing, mutation, missing COW or rebinding, call-contract divergence,
153   and inconsistent byte bindings. It cannot detect a producer that omits or fabricates a coherent trace,
154
      attest the host or compiled kernel/autotuning choice, or establish unrecorded end-to-end semantics.
      Receipt-complete below means complete only for mandatory records and predicates inside this
155
      boundary.
156
157   The checklist has seven audit targets. A target may be fully witnessed, partially witnessed, or left
158   open; passing the case study must not silently upgrade a partial witness into a proof.
159
160
            1. Frozen identity. Model, data, query bank, layer geometry, kernel descriptor, and protocol
               hashes are fixed before execution.
161
            2. Prefix immutability. KℓD and GD  ℓ have identical digests before and after all continuations.

162
            3. Private ownership. KV reservations of distinct shared-document requests are disjoint in
163            the common arena; independent materialized arenas have disjoint storage. At setup, a GDN
164            request either owns a materialized base or holds an explicitly read-only persistent-base alias.
165            After its first transition, all 60 mutable tensors must occupy ranges disjoint from the base
166            and every peer.
167         4. Tail-safe append. If the last document page is partial, its valid tokens are copied once to a
168            private page before a request appends.
169
            5. Dispatch provenance. Both controls invoke the same recorded attention Python callable
170
               with the same query, mask, position, and append contracts; fallback and full-KV concate-
171            nation are forbidden. This does not fingerprint a compiled Triton binary or autotuning
172            choice.
173
            6. Cross-arm equivalence. For the same query and schedule, the materialized and shared
174
               ownership arms must have exactly equal generated tokens, full-vocabulary logits, final
175            logical KV, and request recurrent state.
176
            7. Cross-N prefix consistency. For each query observed at two or more fan-outs, its result at
177
               every larger fan-out must match its smallest-N result.
178
179   Targets 2–5 are pre-output ownership and trace obligations, whereas targets 6–7 are relational
180   semantic checks. Matching tokens or logits is therefore insufficient for a passing audit. In the nine
181   executed positive controls, lifecycle/ownership/storage receipts supply the observed first gate for M1
182   and M3–M5, while request-binding/call receipts supply it for M2 and M6–M9. All nine injected
183   paths stop before post-injection semantics; this is bounded first-gate localization, not evidence about
184   unseen-fault coverage.
185
186   Minimal adoption workflow and decision rule (normative). Applying F ORK AUDIT to a sequen-
187   tial hybrid runtime requires four steps. (1) Freeze and assign roles: bind code, model, data, protocol,
188   and the mapping from runtime objects to document, base, request, and private-reservation roles before
189
      candidate outputs. (2) Capture mandatory records: emit setup, first-transition, and final lifecycle
      events together with canonical content digests, normalized storage ranges, ordered append/dispatch
190
      receipts, semantic outputs, and named allocator snapshots. (3) Replay the seven targets blindly:
191
      an independent aggregate recomputes every applicable predicate from frozen records; a target is
192   receipt-complete (RC) only when all mandatory records exist, partial when evidence is sampled
193   or proxy-level, and open when a mandatory record is absent or replay fails. (4) Add fault records
194   only for fault-sensitivity claims: freeze the target-to-gate map before outputs and retain clean false
195   positives, wrong-gate detections, and escapes as valid negatives. The RC label uses the threat model
196   above. In the primary run, terminal identity is RC, while per-call compiled dispatch and autotuning
197   provenance remain partial. We evaluate this workflow on the fixed Qwen runtime below. Appendix D
198   gives the exhaustive target-to-record dependency matrix and the pointer-free storage schema used by
199   this audit.

399
400   The primary factorial executes 8 × 3 × 4 = 96 rank–fan-out–factor configurations. Each configuration

406   replay additionally reconstructs all 288 adjacent-fan-out comparisons—N = 1 → 8 and 8 → 32
407   for every arm, rank, and shared query identity—with no mismatch. These are relational equalities
408
      among the registered cells: they support the receipt-level ownership witness but cannot by themselves
      exclude a defect shared by every arm.
409
410   The range witnesses explain where that equality comes from. A borrowed GDN cell begins with
411   exactly 60 immutable persistent-base aliases per request; a materialized cell begins request–base

413   private mutable tensors whose byte ranges are disjoint from the persistent base and all completed
414
      peers. Incomplete borrowed requests remain exact read-only aliases until their own transition.
      These predicates pass at setup, first transition, and post-generation for every factor configuration.
415
      In particular, the N = 1 cells directly exercise request–base disjointness rather than relying on a
416
      vacuous peer comparison.

507   8   C ONCLUSION
508
509   For one fixed sequential Qwen3.5 stack, F ORK AUDIT turns output agreement into a stricter replayable
510   ownership claim. Under honest producer capture, a run passes only when registered outputs agree and
511   the receipt ledger witnesses immutable document state, disjoint request/base and request/peer storage,
512   tail-safe append, first-transition recurrent rebinding, and recorded call contracts. Selected operator
513   oracles reduce, but do not eliminate, common-mode risk, while designed mutants demonstrate
514
      bounded first-gate localization. The case study therefore establishes the feasibility and diagnostic
      value of this audit boundary in the specified stack. Independent producer recapture, compiled-dispatch
515
      attestation, concurrent execution, and end-to-end correctness remain outside that result.
