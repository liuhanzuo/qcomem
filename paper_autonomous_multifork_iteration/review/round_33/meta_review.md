# Round 33 synthesis (not an additional reviewer score)

## Outcome

The three identical, independent PDF-only Terra reviews score the frozen paper
`4, 4, 4`, all at confidence `4`; all three verdicts are Reject.  The new R33
evidence is recognized as internally coherent and useful, but does not change
the panel's acceptance decision.

## Shared positives

- All three recognize the output-equality gap and the value of phase-aware,
  fail-closed ownership receipts.
- All three regard the contract, pointer-free storage schema, coverage/verdict
  separation, and scope discipline as technically strong.
- All three acknowledge the 96-cell factorial, gate-suppression comparison,
  separately designed five-fault campaign, and process-separated GDN observer
  as meaningful evidence.
- No reviewer reports a numerical inconsistency, incorrect table, unreadable
  figure, citation problem, or PDF layout defect.

## Remaining decision blockers

- The dominant blocker remains trusted producer-side slot enumeration.  The
  out-of-process observer removes same-process reconstruction but deliberately
  does not independently enumerate model/KV state or establish OS/driver truth.
- The fixed single-model/H20, sequential batch-one scope is still considered
  too narrow for broad systems significance.
- Five PDF-only designed faults are viewed as stronger than the disclosed
  gate-designed controls, but not as natural-defect coverage or a false-negative
  estimate; no common fault corpus or conventional testing baseline is present.
- The integration is viewed as disciplined engineering unless adoption cost,
  overhead, or comparative defect-finding value is demonstrated.
- One reviewer additionally identifies excessive RR2/R28/R30/R33 labeling and
  density as a repairable presentation problem.

## Revision implication

The trusted-capture, external-validity, natural-defect, and overhead concerns
require material new evidence to resolve.  A bounded writing-only revision can
still improve focus by replacing internal round labels with descriptive cohort
names and stating the non-adversarial capture-adapter threat model more directly.
It should not reintroduce noncentral unfavorable cost numbers into the main
claim hierarchy merely to answer a practicality question.
