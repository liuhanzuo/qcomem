                                                  A BSTRACT
012            Data attribution is computed from a selected set although optimization follows a
013            schedule. We distinguish set-ordered from path-ordered marginal value by mea-
014            suring each target at multiple entry positions within the same seed. On two frozen
015            synthetic logistic carriers the paired test returns carrier-dependent readings: the
016            sine carrier shows a negative paired departure (g = −0.186, CI[−0.258, −0.115])
017
               that reverses, rather than reproduces, the cross-sectional sign (negative g: early en-
               try lowers value; a negative τ : early-scheduled targets rank higher; Section 4.2),
018
               while the linear carrier is ambiguous (g = +0.010, CI[−0.112, +0.132]). On a
019
               pure set-ordered ridge where value is order-invariant by construction, the identical
020            paired estimator carries a non-negative bias (g = +0.102, CI[+0.028, +0.176]
021            on the canonical allocation, positive on all four allocations, minimum CI lower
022            −0.066). Because a non-negative bias cannot produce a negative departure, the
023            calibration is conservative with respect to the sine headline. The canonical al-
024            location literally ﬁres the sealed binary contract (CI excludes 0), but its conse-
025            quence clause is two-sided (sign-blind) and the measured bias is opposite in sign
026            to the headline, so it does not falsify the negative sine reading (a speciﬁcation erra-
027            tum, not a rewrite of the frozen criterion). Value-conditioned routing reproduces
028
               the cross-sectional B2 negative τ on a set-ordered world, so B2 is a reference-
               conditioned stress test; identiﬁcation remains undecided on this zero-GPU bed.

031   1   I NTRODUCTION
032
      Which training samples matter is often treated as a property of a selected set. Datamodels predict
033
      model outputs from training-set membership (Ilyas et al., 2022), while TRAK provides scalable
034
      set-indexed model-behavior attribution (Park et al., 2023). Neither representation retains the path
035   by which samples entered an optimization trajectory. This abstraction is useful, but it leaves open a
036   prior measurement question: is per-sample marginal value actually invariant to entry order?
037   The question matters whenever data are introduced by a curriculum, sampler, or staged acquisition
038   rule. If value is set-ordered, an order-invariant attribution is an adequate description of sample
039   ranking. If value is path-ordered, the same sample set can induce different marginal-value relations
040   under different entry schedules. Distinguishing these possibilities requires more than observing a
041   nonzero correlation: the experiment must specify the entry construction, use the seed rather than the
042   item as its inference unit, enforce a minimum effect size, and measure the detector’s behavior on a
043   known set-ordered case (Section 4.3) rather than assume it is quiet.
044   We introduce a frozen Construction-Sensitivity Test on a deterministic logistic-regression test bed.
045   Figure 1 emphasizes the controlled comparison (schematic; all quantitative evidence comes from
046   the frozen runs and the data-derived Figure 2): the same sample identities and held-out readout are
047
      used across three entry rules, each evaluated through both a path-sensitive leave-one-out value and
      an order-invariant set comparator.
048
049   Contributions. First, we give a same-sample measurement of per-target entry-position sensitivity,
050   so a cross-sectional selection signal can no longer masquerade as a path effect. Second, a quantiﬁed
051   calibration: the sine carrier shows a negative paired departure (g = −0.186, CI[−0.258, −0.115])
052   that reverses the cross-sectional sign (Section 4.2), the linear carrier is ambiguous (g = +0.010,
053   CI[−0.112, +0.132]), and a set-ordered ridge counter-arm shows the identical paired estimator car-
      ries a non-negative bias (g = +0.102, CI[+0.028, +0.176] on the canonical allocation, positive

      3.1   E NTRY CONSTRUCTIONS
138   Construction A inserts the complete target block from one of the three schedule locations onward
139   and computes leave-one-out value within that block. Construction B1 assigns each target a distinct
140   single injection step, linearly spread from 0.15T to 0.85T . Construction B2 ranks targets by the
141   ﬁxed set-valued reference score, partitions that order into thirds, and injects the high-, middle-, and
142   low-ranked regions at the early, middle, and late locations. Thus, B1 genuinely changes per-sample
143   position, while B2 additionally imposes a region-lumped relation between reference value and entry
144
      time. The comparison identiﬁes the entry rule, not the binary attribute “position was manipulated.”
145   Worked example of Construction A (for Eq. (1)). For the 40 targets Gk and insertion fraction
146   t ∈ {0.15T, 0.50T, 0.85T }, sA (z) = 0 for targets outside the chosen block and sA (z) = ⌊tT ⌋ for
147   targets in the block, so the block is injected only at step ⌊tT ⌋ (all other targets present from step 0).
148   The leave-one-out value is then VA,k (z) = U (w+G ) − U (w−z ), where both ﬁts use the same WSD
149   budget and differ only by whether z is in the block—exactly the general deﬁnition of Eq. (1).

151   3.2   C ONTROLS , VALIDITY, AND FROZEN DECISION
152   The clean negative control uses a strictly convex quadratic ridge substrate with additive, schedule-
153   invariant inﬂuence. Independent random position labels are tested with a per-seed critical value
154   Bonferroni-adjusted over the 20 trials; the frozen acceptance condition is no ﬁres. The decisive
155   result is then read per cell using both the 4-state label and exceeds_threshold. A global
156   construction-dependent verdict requires that the threshold-crossing pattern vary by construction or
157   carrier; it does not license a universal mechanism statement.
158   The code, speciﬁcations, and outputs were frozen before this writing pass. In particular, the current
159   paper does not regenerate or tune the six cells, choose seeds after observing effects, or replace the
160   seed-level uncertainty with item-level pseudo-replication. The provenance manifest and hashes are
161   listed in Appendix B.

350   reliability of the bias sign, not to rewrite the already-ﬁred frozen binary criterion. We deliberately do
351   not report a magnitude-offset ﬁgure (such as −0.288) as a headline, because the offset’s magnitude
352   is not transferable from the ridge substrate to the sine carrier; only its sign is.
353   Framing choice (settled here). We do not adopt a “re-baseline” framing that subtracts the ridge
354   offset magnitude from the sine reading, because that magnitude would need to transfer across carri-
355   ers (ridge → sine, untested, unclaimed). We adopt the sign-only, non-transport conservative claim:
356   a non-negative set-ordered bias cannot explain a negative sine departure. The cost is that we do not
357   assert a quantitative clean-null (bias-corrected) calibration of the sine reading. A future re-baseline
358
      with magnitude transfer would require a carrier-internal order-invariant control (an order-invariant
      variant of the sine, linear-GD, WSD carrier from the same feature process), pre-registered here as a
359
      minimal 0-GPU follow-up rather than run, as it lies outside the frozen evidence.

      7    R ELATED WORK
433
434   Set-indexed data attribution. Datamodels predict behavior from training-set membership (Ilyas
435   et al., 2022), while TRAK aggregates projected training gradients (Park et al., 2023). Both are
436   indexed by selected sets, with no schedule-position axis. We test whether empirical leave-one-out
437   value has a schedule-dependent component that such scores omit, varying entry rules while holding
438   target identities and held-out readout ﬁxed.
439
      Uncertainty and reproducibility. Machine-learning conclusions can be sensitive to statistical
440
      power, run-to-run variance, and reporting choices (Card et al., 2020; Bouthillier et al., 2021; Dodge
441
      et al., 2019; Reimers & Gurevych, 2017). We therefore ﬁx the seed as the inference unit, show
442   conﬁdence intervals rather than a single run, and keep a minimum-effect decision distinct from
443   signiﬁcance. Training-trajectory suites motivate retaining checkpoints and schedules as ﬁrst-class
444   experimental objects (Biderman et al., 2023); our contribution isolates one narrower object, sam-
445   ple entry construction. The frozen analysis plan follows the broader rationale for preregistration in
446   empirical NLP and machine learning (van Miltenburg et al., 2021).

448   8    L IMITATIONS AND SCOPE
449
450   The evidence is restricted to deterministic CPU logistic-regression carriers with synthetic conﬂicts;
451   the registered sine carrier does not represent general nonlinear models or large language models.
452
      The primary arm is the paired test; B2 is only a routing-reproducible stress test. The same-sample
      paired test’s bias on the set-ordered ridge is non-negative (g = +0.102, CI[+0.028, +0.176] on
453
      the canonical allocation, positive on all four allocations, Section 4.3); we report this as a quanti-
454
      ﬁed calibration and disclose that the canonical allocation literally ﬁres the sealed binary contract,
455   whose sign-blind consequence clause is a speciﬁcation erratum that does not falsify the negative
456   sine departure. Linear is not identiﬁed; the earlier selection-confound claim rested on a corrected
457   sign-consistency bug. The decoupled control’s 87.5% power is not evidence for canonical B2.
458   The comparator is an inverse-Hessian GLM reduction, not end-to-end TRAK/datamodel; its τAB
459   values lack frozen uncertainty and are descriptive. One pre-registered set-ordered negative control
460   cannot exclude every artifact or calibrate natural data. A natural carrier, full same-bed baselines, and
461   agreement uncertainty remain necessary and unclaimed.
462
463   Future work: bed transfer to a larger carrier. Transfer the same three-entry-rule, same-seed,
464   same-sample design to a natural-data or LLM-ﬁnetuning carrier, re-running the negative control,
465
      four-state margin readout, and bed certiﬁcate. The carrier-agnostic contract and τa = 0.10 are pre-
      registered, preventing outcome tailoring. This tests nonconvex, diverse-data transfer; it does not
466
      replace same-bed baseline positioning or fresh review.

      9    C ONCLUSION
470
      Whether per-sample marginal value is path-ordered or set-ordered is a carrier-dependent measure-
471   ment, not a universal property, and this controlled, zero-GPU bed leaves the question honestly un-
472   decided. A frozen, pre-registered same-sample paired test controls the value-conditioned routing
473   confound and returns carrier-dependent readings: the sine carrier shows a negative paired depar-
474   ture (g = −0.186, CI[−0.258, −0.115]) that reverses the cross-sectional sign (early entry lowers
475   value; negative cross-sectional τ ranks early-scheduled targets higher), while the linear carrier is
476   ambiguous (g = +0.010, CI[−0.112, +0.132]). A set-ordered ridge counter-arm quantiﬁes the es-
477   timator’s calibration: its bias is non-negative (g = +0.102, CI[+0.028, +0.176] on the canonical
478   allocation, positive on all four allocations), so it cannot explain—and is conservative with respect
479
      to—the negative sine departure. The canonical allocation literally ﬁres the sealed binary contract,
      whose sign-blind consequence clause is a speciﬁcation erratum that does not falsify the negative sine
480
      reading; we disclose the letter-ﬁring and do not rewrite the frozen criterion. Its magnitude remains
481
      uncalibrated across carriers. Value-conditioned routing reproduces the full B2 magnitude on a set-
482   ordered world, making B2 a stress test; set-ordering remains a coarse-graining, not a universally
483   decidable property on this evidence.
