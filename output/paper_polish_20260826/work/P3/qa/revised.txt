011                                               A BSTRACT
012
013            Data attribution is usually indexed by a selected set even when optimization fol-
014            lows a schedule. We test whether marginal value changes with a target’s entry
015            position by measuring the same target at multiple positions within each seed. On
016            two frozen synthetic logistic carriers, the paired estimator gives different read-
017            ings: the registered sine carrier has a negative departure (g = −0.186, 95% CI
018
               [−0.258, −0.115]), whereas the linear carrier is ambiguous (g = +0.010, CI
               [−0.112, +0.132]). The paired sign must not be conflated with the cross-sectional
019
               Kendall sign: negative g means that moving the same target earlier lowers its value,
020
               while negative cross-sectional τ ranks earlier-scheduled targets higher. A pure
021            set-ordered ridge counter-arm reveals a non-negative estimator bias (g = +0.102,
022            CI [+0.028, +0.176] on the canonical allocation; positive point estimates on all
023            four allocations). The canonical allocation therefore fires the sealed two-sided
024            binary contract, but the positive bias cannot generate the negative sine departure;
025            no magnitude correction is transported across carriers. Value-conditioned routing
026            also reproduces the cross-sectional B2 signal in a set-ordered world. The study
027            thus provides a controlled, carrier-dependent measurement, not a general decision
028            between path-ordered and set-ordered value.

031   1   I NTRODUCTION
032
033   Set-indexed attribution methods predict model behavior from training-set membership (Ilyas et al.,
034   2022; Park et al., 2023), but they do not retain the path by which samples entered optimization. This
035   abstraction leaves a prior measurement question: is per-sample marginal value invariant to entry
036   order? If it is, an order-invariant attribution can summarize sample ranking. If it is not, the same
037   sample set can induce different marginal-value relations under different entry schedules.
038   Distinguishing these cases requires more than a nonzero correlation. The entry construction must be
039   explicit; the seed, rather than the item, must be the inference unit; a minimum effect must be separated
040   from statistical significance; and the estimator must be measured on a known set-ordered case. We
041   implement this contract in a deterministic logistic-regression bed. The same sample identities and
042   held-out readout are evaluated under three entry rules, using both a path-sensitive leave-one-out value
043   and an order-invariant comparator.
044   The result has three parts. First, same-sample pairing prevents a cross-sectional selection signal from
045   being read as a path effect. Second, the sine carrier shows a negative paired departure while the
046   linear carrier remains ambiguous. Third, the set-ordered ridge counter-arm exposes a non-negative
047   paired-estimator bias, and a separate routing experiment shows that B2 is a reference-conditioned
048   stress test. These results are limited to two synthetic carriers.

206   4.3       S ET- ORDERED RIDGE COUNTER - ARM
207
208   The identical paired estimator is run on a strictly convex set-ordered ridge whose marginal value is
209   order-invariant by construction. The sealed contract intended a 16-seed CI covering zero to count as
210   silent and any CI excluding zero to count as a fire.
211   The canonical ridge allocation literally fires the sealed binary rule. Across all four allocations,
212   however, point estimates are positive; 14 of 16 canonical seeds are positive, and dropping the most
213   extreme seed of either sign preserves the positive direction. The contract’s sign-blind consequence
214   clause is therefore an erratum: a positive set-ordered bias cannot produce the negative sine departure.
215   This is a sign-only conservative argument. We do not subtract the ridge magnitude from the sine
      value because cross-carrier transport is untested.

303   6    R ELATED W ORK
304
305
      Datamodels and TRAK are indexed by selected sets rather than schedule position (Ilyas et al.,
306
      2022; Park et al., 2023). Our narrower question is whether empirical leave-one-out value has a
307
      schedule-dependent component when identities and held-out readout remain fixed. Machine-learning
308   conclusions can be sensitive to power, run variance, and reporting choices (Card et al., 2020;
309   Bouthillier et al., 2021; Dodge et al., 2019; Reimers & Gurevych, 2017); we therefore use seeds
310   as inference units and separate confidence against zero from a minimum-effect decision. Training-
311   trajectory suites motivate treating schedules as first-class experimental objects (Biderman et al., 2023),
312   and the frozen analysis follows the rationale for preregistration in NLP (van Miltenburg et al., 2021).

315   7    L IMITATIONS AND S COPE
316
317
      The evidence is limited to deterministic CPU logistic-regression carriers with synthetic conflicts; the
318   registered sine key does not represent general nonlinear models or language models. The paired test is
319   primary, while B2 is a routing-reproducible stress test. Its set-ordered calibration has a non-negative
320   bias, and the canonical allocation fires the sealed two-sided rule; only the sign-based conservative
321   conclusion is supported. Linear is not identified. The comparator is an inverse-Hessian GLM
322   reduction, not end-to-end TRAK or datamodels, and its agreement values lack frozen uncertainty. A
323   single set-ordered negative control cannot exclude every artifact or calibrate natural data. Transfer
      requires a natural carrier, fresh controls, full same-bed baselines, and agreement uncertainty.

      8    C ONCLUSION
325
326
      The same-sample paired test gives carrier-dependent readings: the sine carrier has a negative departure
327   (g = −0.186, CI [−0.258, −0.115]) whose meaning reverses the cross-sectional sign, whereas the
328   linear carrier is ambiguous (g = +0.010, CI [−0.112, +0.132]). The set-ordered ridge counter-arm
329   has non-negative bias across four allocations, so it cannot explain the negative sine departure, but the
330   canonical allocation fires the sealed sign-blind binary rule and no cross-carrier magnitude correction is
331   justified. Because routing reproduces the B2 cross-sectional signal on a set-ordered world, B2 remains
332   a stress test. These synthetic results do not decide whether data value is generally path-ordered or
333   set-ordered.
