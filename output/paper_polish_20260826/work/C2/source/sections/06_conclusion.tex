\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

CoMem provides a factorized measurement of retrieval and cached depth.  On the
tested Qwen3-8B configurations, fixed top-$k$ retrieval keeps the model read short
as document length grows, while the external activation/text store grows linearly.
At the measured 6.7k-token read, moving from $j{=}0$ to $j{=}12$ saves only
0.3\,GB and reduces qa1 read-out fidelity under the registered comparisons.  The
five-arm study retains this loss after freezing the named encoding and relocation
factors, subject to the stated lower-band context-set boundary.  The reusable output
is the matched protocol and its negative depth-cost curve; end-to-end value including
decode, and recovery after cache-aware training, remain unmeasured.
