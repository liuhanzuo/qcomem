\paragraph{The fixed-read quality cost is task-dependent.}
The $j{=}0{-}j{=}12$ gap is not length-uniform and the fixed top-$k$ read is
task-dependent: on the matching-style RULER probes (Table
\ref{tab:ruler-percell}) the fixed read retains $90$--$100\%$ recall, and on
the single-supporting-fact qa1 sweep (Table~\ref{tab:depthcurve}) deeper reads
degrade with cached depth.  The multi-hop qa2 composition probe
(Table~\ref{tab:composition-app}) is \emph{not} load-bearing: under the
registered adjudicator its overall verdict is not evaluable (the
$j{=}12$ load arm falls below the $\geq k^*{=}24$ floor at 8k), so no clean
``$j{=}0$ loses $X$\,pp'' quantification is registered for composition.
Consistent with the qa2 panel's negative-control and floor-gated cells and with
the author re-forward versus \texttt{dense} disclosure above, we do not claim that
a fixed read preserves accuracy on compositional or multi-hop queries; the
main-text quality claim is scoped to single-supporting-fact, needle-style
tasks.

\subsection{Truncation envelope derivation}
\label{app:truncation-envelope}
Table~\ref{tab:invariance-diagnostics} collects the prefix-pinned arm ranges
and the resulting two-cap envelopes.  To bound readout identity without
treating it as known, the registered worst-case construction assigns each
unresolved missing-answer row adversarially per arm: a lower endpoint pairs the
smallest $j{=}0$ reading with the largest $j{=}12$ reading, and the upper
endpoint uses the reverse extremes.  Neither envelope contains zero, so this
construction preserves the sign of the pinned result.  Both envelopes use only
the qa1 decisive samples and reader; they are not point estimates and do not
replace the retained convention band in Table~\ref{tab:readdepth-decisive}.

\noindent\begin{minipage}{\textwidth}
Table~\ref{tab:invariance-diagnostics}'s at-least-$+18$\,pp headroom result is a
different estimand from the envelope above: rather than assigning the unresolved
rows adversarially, it conditions on a particular recovery rule (the post-hoc
stripped reader at cap 512) and reports the smallest headroom consistent with
that rule, leaving at least $+18$\,pp.  The per-row discordant counts and the
resulting bound are given in Table~\ref{tab:invariance-diagnostics}.  The two
constructions therefore have different scopes, but both agree in sign with the
pinned direction.
\end{minipage}

\paragraph{Code, data, and artifact availability.}
Write/read, selection, and RULER evaluation live in the modules
\texttt{comem.model}, \texttt{comem.selectors}, and \texttt{eval.ruler};
all three quantitative figures are produced by deterministic scripts in the
manuscript build tree, and the exact running inputs are enumerated in the
protocol matrix (Table~\ref{tab:protocol-matrix}).  An anonymized artifact
accompanies review rather than waiting for acceptance: it contains the write,
read, selector, scorer, and evaluation drivers, the frozen readers, the
preregistration documents, the per-arm scored CSVs behind every table in this
paper, and the figure scripts, with all account-bound path identifiers removed.
Hash convention: every identifier printed in this paper is a truncated SHA-256
prefix, and the artifact manifest lists the full 64-hex digest for each file it
ships, so a reviewer can extend any prefix below to a complete digest.  Two
identifiers cannot be extended and we do not pad them: the tokenizer artifact
survives in the run log only at six hex characters (\texttt{5a7303}), and the
pre-repair \texttt{572907d7} driver blob is not recoverable from history, so its
difference against the repaired driver is archived as a diff rather than
reproduced.

\paragraph{Cross-trial harness provenance (E2 five-arm deck).}
The five-arm decomposition merges two trials: A/D/R/C come from Trial
1895844 and the depth-only arm E from the repair trial 1896955 (different
harness snapshots: \texttt{eval/babilong.py} \texttt{572907d7} and
\texttt{7f4aa8aa} respectively).  \texttt{comem/model.py} is bit-identical
across both (\texttt{0e7ef9e6}); the only \texttt{babilong.py} difference is
the R243 fix adding \texttt{depth\_only} to the \texttt{--ablation} option
\emph{choices} list (archived as docs/C2\_R243\_babilong\_depth\_only\_diff.txt).
This is a driver CLI-whitelist edit: \texttt{args.ablation} is consumed at a
single point (\texttt{babilong.py:231}) and the choices are bare string
literals with no reflection, so arms A/D/R/C (which already accept
\texttt{none}/\texttt{relocation\_only}/\texttt{chunk\_indep\_only}) have no
reachable behavioral difference across the two snapshots, and the
\texttt{sample\_id} (=sha256(input+prompt+question)[:16]) sequences are
bit-identical between the two trials ($n{=}100$ each length).  The reported
scorer (\texttt{babilong.metrics.compare\_answers}, pinned \texttt{f77c1398})
operates downstream of generation and does not control this gap; the static
whitelist argument above is the side-effect certificate.  A caveat is that the
original \texttt{572907d7} blob is not recoverable from git, so the diff is
archived, not reproduced from history.

\paragraph{Pairing-identity verification.}
For the historical decisive qa1 cells, a column literally named
\texttt{context\_sha256} was never emitted, and earlier versions of this work
therefore rested pairing on the registered per-arm \texttt{sample\_id}
sequences and the deterministic oracle selector alone.  That gap is now closed
one level further: \texttt{sample\_id} is itself the 16-hex \texttt{sha256}
prefix of the full formatted model input, so the preregistered content-level
join was recomputed on it for every decisive cell, with a complete $100/100$
overlap and no orphaned, duplicated, or question-mismatched context
(Appendix~\ref{app:pairing-provenance}).  Pairing therefore no longer rests on
row order, and it never rested on the repeated question string (which is not
unique).  What we still do not claim is a join against a separately stored
64-hex digest of the raw document text, which was not retained.  This historical
three-arm check is distinct from, and complemented by, the five-arm
decomposition pairing described in Appendix~\ref{app:protocol-full}.  E2 is
different by construction: its key is
\texttt{sha256(context+prompt+question)[:16]}, and the independently checked
A/D/R/C/E files carry the same ordered 100 unique keys at each length.  No
paired claim is made across 4k and 16k, which are different length cells.

\paragraph{Matched-memory scope and preregistered scale check.}
At 128k and fixed $k=12$, E1 records \textit{read\_len}=6,657 and
median-of-three peaks of 18.2\,GB for $j=0$ and 17.9\,GB for $j=12$ in each of
two trials (six of six repeats agree at 0.1\,GB print precision).  The measured
0.3\,GB difference is the read-length-scoped analytic quantity
$(12/36)B_{\mathrm{KV}}\,\textit{read\_len}$, not evidence that depth caching is
generally free.  We preregister a $k=48$ matched check: because the analytic
band scales linearly with read length, it predicts approximately 1.2\,GB of
depth-attributed saving.  That check has not been run and is not a result of
this paper.

\subsection{Interval constructions for the decision cells}
\label{app:paired-cis}
The pinned rows of Tables~\ref{tab:readdepth-main}
and~\ref{tab:readdepth-decisive} carry the primary
magnitude.  Their paired risk difference is $(c-b)/n$; the exact conditional
interval applies Clopper--Pearson to $c/(b+c)$ and rescales it as
$(2p-1)\,s/n$, where $s=b+c$ and $p=c/s$ (registered inputs are in
Table~\ref{tab:protocol-matrix}).  It conditions on the observed discordant
pair count and remains the primary interval; Table~\ref{tab:paired-cis} also
reports the wider Newcombe Method~11 cross-check, which corrects the
square-and-add construction by the observed pairing correlation.  Earlier
versions of this appendix omitted the pairing-free construction on the grounds
that it discards the paired structure; we now report it deliberately as the
assumption-free floor, because the raw contexts of these historical cells cannot
be re-audited by a reviewer.  Setting the pairing correlation to zero in the
same square-and-add formula gives an interval that uses only the two marginals,
so it holds whatever the pairing is, and the accompanying unpaired
two-proportion test gives $z=3.924$, $p=8.7\times10^{-5}$ at the mnt160
decision cell.  The ordering of the three rows in
Table~\ref{tab:paired-cis} is the expected one: conditioning on discordance is
tightest, the correlation-corrected interval is intermediate, and the
pairing-free interval is widest.
