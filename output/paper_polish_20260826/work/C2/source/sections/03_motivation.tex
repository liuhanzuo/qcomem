\section{Experimental Protocol and Claim Map}
\label{sec:protocol}

Everything in this paper is measured on a frozen, training-free stack: the
36-layer Qwen3-8B backbone~\citep{qwen3} with chunk size $c=512$ and CoMem split
$j=12$, in bfloat16, with no parameter update anywhere in the pipeline.  The qa1
and RULER depth studies use a fixed $k=12$; the LongEval study uses the per-task
BM25 settings of Appendix Table~\ref{tab:protocol-matrix} ($k\in\{4,6\}$), so its
quality gap is not a $k{=}12$ operating-point claim.  The surviving artifacts
document no clean validation split for the earlier best-$k$ sweeps, so rather than
select $k$ on the test set we exclude those values from headline claims: our
result is a single fixed-configuration test, not a claim that $k=12$ is optimal.
How the depth cost changes with $k$ is unknown, and a matched $k$-sweep at the 4k
decision cell remains future work.

Table~\ref{tab:claim-map} is the map a reviewer should read first: each headline
claim, its estimand, the control that identifies that estimand, the scope in
which we assert it, and where its evidence lives.  We report three estimands and
never merge them: the read-depth \emph{direction}
(Section~\ref{sec:readdepth}), the depth \emph{attribution}
(Section~\ref{sec:attribution}), and the \emph{serving} account
(Section~\ref{sec:serving}).  The remaining protocol detail --- the RULER,
LongEval and read-out-invariance studies, the depth-sweep protocol, the reader
taxonomy, the five-arm cross-trial audit, and the reproducibility record --- is in
Appendices~\ref{app:protocol-full} and~\ref{app:repro}.

\paragraph{Matched read-depth study (direction estimand).}
To isolate depth from retrieval, the decisive BABILong qa1
study~\citep{babilong} uses an oracle selector, $k=12$, and the same selected
chunks, query, greedy decoder, and Qwen3-8B checkpoint for $j=12$ and $j=0$;
each registered context bucket contains positionally paired examples under the
sample counts and generation caps of Appendix
Table~\ref{tab:protocol-matrix}.  Paired differences use the two-sided exact
McNemar test~\citep{mcnemar}, with $b$ the $j{=}12$-correct/$j{=}0$-wrong pairs
and $c$ the reverse; the frozen primary reader extracts the first task label and
the sensitivity reader first strips a leading model-generated option enumeration
(full reader taxonomy in Appendix~\ref{app:protocol-full}).  Pairing is verified
by content hash: the registered \texttt{sample\_id} is the 16-hex
\texttt{sha256} prefix of the full formatted model input, and re-running the
preregistered content-level join on it makes every decisive cell a complete
$100/100$ overlap with no orphaned, duplicated, or question-mismatched context,
reproducing every published cell exactly
(Appendix~\ref{app:pairing-provenance}); a separately stored 64-hex document
digest was never retained and is not claimed.

\paragraph{Five-arm mechanism decomposition (attribution estimand).}
At fixed $j=12$, length, selector, and decoder, we compare: A, the matched
$j=0$ full re-forward; D, deployed chunk-local write plus fresh-contiguous
read; R, chunk-local write with original document positions at read; C,
contiguous cross-chunk write with fresh-contiguous read; and E, contiguous
cross-chunk write with original document positions.  Thus E freezes both
chunk-independent encoding and relocation when compared with A; the remaining
lower-band context-set difference is stated with the attribution result rather than
treated as eliminated.  Each 4k/16k arm has $n=100$ and uses the official
\texttt{babilong.metrics.compare\_answers} qa1 scorer, Wilson intervals, and
two-sided exact McNemar contrasts on the same content-hash key
\texttt{sample\_id}.  Driver pins, the one-line argument repair, and the
cross-trial audit of that deck are in Appendices~\ref{app:protocol-full}
and~\ref{app:repro}.  No other experimental arm is introduced.

\paragraph{Serving study (serving estimand).}
The systems measurements use the same Qwen3-8B backbone on one H20 with
97.8\,GB available memory, and all GPU peaks and prefill measurements are
median-of-three.  We distinguish (i) full-context rereading, (ii) retrieved
raw-token recomputation at $j=0$, and (iii) CoMem at $j=12$.  The registered
record reports write, per-query read, GPU peak, and RULER/LongEval quality, but
no end-to-end latency including decode, so the recorded read/prefill endpoints
are tabulated as endpoints and never interpolated.  We therefore make the
framing explicit: the system's benefit is a \emph{retrieval} benefit (a
fixed-$k$ short read instead of a full-context read)---an operating point a
matched $j{=}0$ short read also delivers without any depth cache---and depth
caching is measured as a \emph{quality} cost, not a saving.  The matched $j=0$
versus
$j=12$ direction remains the load-bearing intervention; the depth curve locates
$j{=}12$ within an overall downward trend (no tested deeper working point
recovers accuracy), without asserting a strictly monotone curve.
