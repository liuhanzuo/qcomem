\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{The system is not constant-memory.}
The fixed-$k$ pack is bounded, but GPU peak is not constant: it stays nearly
flat through 128k and rises materially at the separate \mbox{one-million-token}
serving point (Appendix~\ref{app:repro}).  The activation/text/index stores
grow as $O(\text{context})$; index size and BM25 latency remain unmeasured.

\paragraph{Depth caching loses information needed by the deployed read.}
The same-retrieval $j=12$ arm is worse than $j=0$ on qa1 and the registered
RULER/LongEval aggregates, and no tested deeper point recovers $j{=}0$ accuracy
(Appendix Table~\ref{tab:depthcurve}); identical-position split/rejoin does not
validate relocated chunks.  The answer-anchored 512-cap result is underpowered
and the directional diagnostics are post-hoc, leaving a clean magnitude open.
The quality cost is also task-dependent: the fixed read retains near-perfect
recall on needle-style single-supporting-fact probes, so that claim does not
extend to multi-hop composition, which is not evaluable under the registered
adjudicator (Appendix~\ref{app:secondary}).  The 16k dense/re-forward controls
are a non-rejection, not an equivalence test, so a retrieval-side deficit of a
few pp is not excluded, though it remains far below the \emph{depth} effect.

\paragraph{The result is training-free and therefore bounded.}
Every number here comes from a frozen backbone with no parameter update, so the
measured depth cost is what a \emph{training-free} deployment pays.  It is not a
statement about what is achievable in principle: representations can be trained
to be more cache-compressible~\citep{kvcat}, and a backbone or adapter optimized
for reading relocated mid-depth states could recover part of the gap we measure.
We ran no such training, so we neither claim nor exclude that recovery.

\paragraph{Selection, uncertainty, and scorer resolution.}
The archived best-$k$ sweeps lack a validation/test separation, so we report
fixed $k=12$; primary RULER and qa1 cells have $n=50$ and $n=100$ under one
checkpoint and seed, and overlapping half-splits diagnose resampling stability,
not independent replication.  The pairing join is over the formatted model input
rather than an independently stored 64-hex document digest, which was never
retained, so the load-bearing cell also carries the pairing-free two-proportion
floor.  The official scorer collapses roughly six in ten raw-generation
differences onto one label, so the $0/100$ endpoint agreement between arms (e.g.\
E and C) is indistinguishability \emph{at label resolution}, not identity or
inertness of a deleted factor (Appendix~\ref{app:extended-limitations}).

\paragraph{Mechanism-attribution boundary of the closest prior.}
\texttt{NOT\_MEASURED}: the attributed-mechanism support of the closest prior citation(s)
is not verified item-by-item by a human reader; the claim of mechanism
attribution to those loci (Layer-Condensed KV, HCache, KV-Direct) is therefore
bounded and not asserted as human-verified.

\paragraph{Serving and data governance.}
Full attention at 128k uses 71.4\,GB and remains feasible on the H20, CoMem
recomputes the upper band per decoded token so 0.68\,s is read/prefill rather
than end-to-end latency, and the 0.3\,GB E1 difference establishes no general
depth-memory or end-to-end benefit.  Raw text and activations require encrypted
storage, access control, and source-bound deletion.
