011                                               A BSTRACT
012
013            Multi-agent research runs produce ﬁre archives: a pre-registration, endpoint code,
014            and reports claiming a verdict. Such verdicts are usually judged from prose, re-
015            runs, or producer attestation. This paper is a transparency report on the construc-
016
               tion of AUDIT-GATE, a static zero-GPU ﬁrst gate built during one live run: what
               was pre-registered, what was amended only after observation and why, what still
017
               stands, and what drifted. Its decision rule returns exactly one of PASS, FAIL or
018
               POSTPONE, and every verdict ships with a re-runnable certiﬁcate; the bundle
019            is an independent re-run interface — a pinned checker, pinned inputs, and one
020            command — that any third party can execute, although this submission does not
021            claim that a third party has already executed it. AUDIT-GATE comprises four
022            archive-check primitives (endpoint reachability, boxed positive controls, manifest
023            three-arm acceptance, unreachable-primary-leg archiving), a ﬁve-ﬁeld endpoint
024            registration rule, a healthy-arm non-degeneracy criterion, and the PRIM5 machine-
025            string-to-state projection. Its ﬁeld audit has two legs: a frozen-checker six-case
026            ledger and a pre-registered decisive cell over seven archives external to the audit-
027
               ing seat but internal to the same run. The decisive cell separates two provenance
               tiers: the frozen auditor-transcription tier has 6 PASS, 1 FAIL and 0 POSTPONE;
028
               after mechanical extraction and current-verdict synchronization, the current tier
029
               has 3 PASS, 2 FAIL and 2 POSTPONE. One calibration remains owed: no inde-
030            pendently labelled population of ﬁre archives exists in the run, so we report no
031            detection or false-alarm rate and instead register the design that would measure
032            them. At the frozen tier, the fourteen ﬁeld units exercise PASS and the FAIL
033            family but not POSTPONE; eight synthetic defect-injected archives exercise that
034            third state and every emitting clause. Every state and clause then has an executed
035            instance except the clause whose zero is its intended reading: an unregistered ver-
036            dict string. The framework tests mechanical operability, not whether a verdict is
037            scientiﬁcally right.

      1    I NTRODUCTION
041
      A ﬁre claim says that a pre-registered decision point ﬁred. In a multi-agent run, such claims arrive
042
      as archives — a frozen pre-registration, consumer scripts, and result reports — and reviewers must
043   decide whether to believe them. The usual instruments drift in the same direction: prose drifts from
044   its artifacts; re-running analysis answers a question about today’s code rather than the registered one;
045   and self-attestation is exactly the channel a reward-seeking agent would use, whose stated reasons
046   need not track the computation performed (Turpin et al., 2023). A ﬁrst gate that persuasive writing
047   cannot pass is missing. Figure 1 states the shape the object took by the end of the run. Our claim
048   is deliberately archaeological: we report how AUDIT-GATE was built inside one live run, including
049   post-observation amendments as ﬁndings of the construction (Tables 12 and 8), not as defects.
050   We observed a sharper, strictly zero-GPU question. Many ﬁre archives fail before any statistical
051   question is even reachable: the registered readout has no computor in any consumer script; the gate
052   reports a rate over an empty denominator; the manifest references artifacts that are simply not on disk
053   and are not marked absent; the “healthy” arm whose zero calibrates a threshold is a deterministic

129   2.2   PRIM1–PRIM4
130
131   PRIM1 (reachability envelope). The endpoint side, readout, and n must be pinned. Let [L, U ]
132
      be the two-sided 95% Clopper–Pearson interval (Clopper & Pearson, 1934) for k successes in n
      trials at α = 0.05, and let θ be the registered threshold. An endpoint is design-decidable when at
133
      its registered n some observable outcome either crosses θ or certiﬁes equivalence, and observed-
134
      decided when the observed interval actually does so:
135
136                LO : U ≤ θ;       HI : L ≥ θ;        ABS (TOST) : [L, U ] ⊆ [θ − δ, θ + δ]
137
      for a pre-registered margin δ > 0. The retired singleton-intersection reading [L, U ] ∩ {θ} trivialized
138   at n = 1: it made every such endpoint automatically “decided.” Under TOST, n = 1 is never
139   automatically observed-decided, and an unregistered δ is a post-hoc zone. An interval satisfying no
140   branch yields POSTPONE with a minimal-reﬁll obligation. For the degenerate zero column k = 0,
141                                                         ( )1/n

144   so the reﬁll is the integer n⋆ = min{ n : U (n) ≤ θ }. The exercised numeric instance and its
145   checked reﬁll are reported in Table 11 (PTSD-2); the runner recomputes n⋆ by search rather than
146   reciting it.
147   PRIM2 (boxed positive control). Every gate must carry at least one must-FAIL arm built from a
148   known-illegal input, boxed away from the primary data, with the acceptance criterion that the must-
149   FAIL arm ﬁres on all of its cases while the anti-pass arm ﬁres on none. A positive control that passes
150   unconditionally has zero evidential strength, and the audit reports FAIL rather than a passing gate.
151
      PRIM3 (manifest three-arm acceptance). For every artifact referenced by a manifest, exactly
152
      one of three arms applies: present with matching hash (PASS); present with drifted hash and no
153   recorded cause (FAIL); absent without an explicit ABSENT marker (FAIL). The third arm is the one
154   that produced this framework’s own constructive self-FAIL, case F3 of Section 3.1; we make no
155   claim that it is the most productive arm, because in our corpus no detecting arm ﬁred more than
156   once (Figure 2). These three arms are the states the ﬁeld corpus produced; the decision is really
157   a function of four record-time facts, and Section 3.3 closes the enumeration at ﬁve states, adding
158   drift-with-a-recorded-cause (PASS) and absent-with-a-marker (POSTPONE).
159   PRIM4 (unreachable primary leg). If the primary leg’s self-test corpus is unreachable in the round
160   being claimed — zero scorable rows, empty manifest — the negative-result archiving verdict holds
161   automatically, and re-labelling the failed primary run as a sensitivity analysis is forbidden.

      2.3   G2: WAS THE ZERO MEASURED OR CONSTRUCTED ?
163
164   Gates that calibrate on “the healthy arm shows zero false alarms” must distinguish a measured
165   zero from a constructed one. A deterministic or zero-dispersion healthy arm carries no threshold
166   information, so the audit emits FAIL_DEGENERATE_HEALTHY_ANCHOR. The current runner
167   earns that verdict from the archived ﬁelds rather than assigning it as the superseded edition did
168   (Table 12). Its same-path probe is an r42 snapshot, not an invariant ﬂoor. A threshold may be
169   repaired before freezing; changing it after observation is a gate swap.

171   2.4   W HERE THE CONSUMPTION VERDICT IS EVALUATED
172
      A recurring ambiguity is when to judge that a primitive was consumed. We bind the evaluation to
173
      the producer’s own pre-registered binding decision point, then to the later of (the consumer’s ﬁrst
174   consuming round, the producer’s release round), plus K observer rounds with K = 2 by default.
175   This single rule excludes the two opposite readings that otherwise coexist: “any action by any seat
176   counts as consumption” and “only the producing seat’s actions count”.

178   2.5   A DVISORY STATUS , DISCLOSED IN FULL
179
180   The fan-in ledger that records consumption evidence carries the structural property cannot_
181   FAIL_by_design = true, and we keep that disclosure in the main text rather than in a footnote.
182   Its meaning is narrow and must not be inﬂated in either direction: the ledger never auto-promotes
183   itself to a mandatory gate, which is not the same as never emitting FAIL. It does emit FAIL ver-

      dicts, and the two on record are a constructive self-FAIL on this framework’s own ledger (four of

      reusable counterfactual template any seat can apply post hoc. A scoreboard is not a gate; we report
186
      it as advisory evidence, never as a blocking condition on another seat.

      2.6   W HAT “ MECHANICALLY OPERABLE ” MEANS
189
190   A verdict is mechanically operable when (a) it follows from ﬁlesystem- or string-level facts, (b) a
191   re-runnable script certiﬁcate recomputes it, and (c) every anchor is pinned to the artifact’s location
192   at the time of record. Condition (c) is not pedantry: an early version of our own audit resolved an
193   anchor to where the ﬁle would be looked up later, and a failure to reproduce at that later path is a
194   stale anchor, not a falsiﬁed original verdict.
195
196   Freeze-discipline disclosure. The ﬁrst-run implementation contradicted the registered diversity
197   predicate and returned FAIL_VACUOUS_STATES; it was corrected only after observing the cell.
198   Worse, that edition was replaced before it was pinned, so the verdict is recorded but not recom-
199
      putable. By our own PRIM3 and certiﬁcate rule this is a self-FAIL of our record-keeping, not a
      repairable footnote. The verdict-state map, PRIM3 enumeration, and G2 predicate also changed
200
      after observation. Table 12 and Appendix C preserve the version-by-version account, including the
201
      record-time/write-time distinction.

      2.7   PRIM5: VERDICT- DOMAIN CLOSURE
204
205   Checkers emit reason-bearing machine strings, not states. PRIM5 therefore registers a map from
206   every emitted string onto exactly one of PASS, FAIL, or POSTPONE and applies it by script. Prove-
207   nance preﬁxes are stripped; an absent string becomes UNMAPPED and makes the certiﬁcate fail;
208   unit and group strings occupy separate codomains; and each entry says how it was exercised. The
209   complete rules and map are in Appendix C; the projection command is in Appendix A.
210
211   What a group verdict does and does not assert. The decisive cell’s group verdict is about au-
212   dit execution, not a conjunction over archive verdicts: it says the audit ran, mapped every string,
213
      exercised its controls, and separated at least two projected states. Its canonical machine string is
      EXECUTION_VALID (renamed from AUDIT_EXECUTION_PASS so the label cannot be misread
214
      as archive operability). Panel B of Table 1 separately reports archive operability and includes a
215
      FAIL; Appendix C preserves the rule and its bidirectional disagreements.

402
403
      The four registered exits remain. PASS is a population-level aspiration: all mechanically unreach-
404
      able endpoints are caught (false-open = 0) and no operable archive is killed (false-kill = 0); a ﬁnite
405   corpus can certify only bounds on those rates. KILL ﬁres if an independently conﬁrmed unreach-
406   able endpoint is judged PASS or the false-kill rate is evidenced above 5%. MERGE withdraws the
407   framework into a survey if it merely restates MLOps governance. STOP-PASS additionally requires
408   one independent external ﬁre claim to adopt the primitives as its pre-creation gate.
409
      Two further candidates came from in-run review (Appendix F). Clause (vii) is falsiﬁed by a gate
410
      that reaches its registered bound, has no post-hoc discriminating power, and is not rejected by (viii).
411   Clause (viii) is falsiﬁed by byte-identical load-bearing ﬁelds that do not make a downstream paired
412   or maximum comparison tautological.
413
414

415
      author with their checkers. Calibration requires a labelled ﬁre-archive population whose operability
416
      status is established independently of us, which does not exist inside a live run.

1166   certify long-generation behaviour; (vi) static inspection cannot see a runtime crash. PRIM2 and
1167   PRIM3 are the mechanical forms of (i) and (iii); (iv) and (v) survive as reasons why a readout-layer
