# P1 structure audit

## Paper identity

This paper reports AUDIT-GATE, a static zero-GPU audit that maps research “fire archives” to PASS, FAIL, or POSTPONE using registered endpoint and archive checks, with replayable certificates and explicit post-observation amendments.

## Claim thread

Archive-verdict drift problem -> endpoint and manifest primitives -> certificate discipline -> two-ledger field audit -> synthetic fixtures for unobserved branches -> calibration gap -> bounded methodological conclusion.

## High-impact issues

1. The title and term “fire-gate meta-primitive” are difficult to parse before the object is defined.
2. The abstract is highly compressed and mixes framework definition, audit outcomes, provenance tiers, fixtures, and limitations in one block.
3. The introduction repeats the archive composition and replay contract before prioritizing the scientific question.
4. “Independent re-run interface” can be misread as independent execution; the manuscript correctly denies third-party execution but should keep that caveat adjacent.
5. The conclusion is embedded in “Open items” and can synthesize the result more directly while retaining unresolved exits.

## Planned repair

Clarify the title and abstract, reduce redundant framing in the introduction, preserve all state-machine and certificate semantics, and tighten the final synthesis. Do not change machine strings, paths, counts, or appendix ledgers.
