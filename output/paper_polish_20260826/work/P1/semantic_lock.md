- Synthetic fixtures exercise POSTPONE and unobserved branches but do not calibrate detection or false-alarm performance on natural archives.
- No independently labelled population or third-party execution exists; v2 calibration is blocked pending at least 118 independently labelled defect-free archives and an external labeller.
