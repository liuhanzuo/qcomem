# C1 structure audit

## Paper identity

This paper develops two construct-matched input-blind references for multiple-choice evaluation: an arm-independent best-constant floor for interface comparison and an arm-conditional, option-count-stratified permutation null for item-level information.

## Claim thread

Problem (nominal chance can reward degenerate interfaces) -> two distinct reporting questions -> v1 and v2 estimands -> legality-aware calibration of the floor -> damaged-arm evidence and diagnostic interventions -> bounded reporting rule and limitations.

## High-impact issues

1. “Gate,” “validity gate,” “reporting floor,” and “necessary test” are used in ways that can appear contradictory even though the intended rule is one-directional.
2. The abstract is dense and delays the simple v1/v2 distinction.
3. The introduction repeats prior-art positioning before clearly stating the empirical findings.
4. Some novelty language around v2 risks sounding broader than the carefully delimited claim in the appendix.
5. The main-text Discussion is too compressed relative to the extensive appendix and does not cleanly restate the scope boundary.

## Planned repair

Clarify “reporting check” versus scientific validity, lead the abstract with the two estimands, tighten contribution statements, and turn the Discussion into a compact synthesis. Preserve all tables, numerical results, calibration corrections, and retraction history.
