This paper shows that identity-lock periodicity measured by an active-block recorder follows configured block length under a composite intervention and proposes a two-layer reporting rule that separates a-priori reachability from measured magnitude.

## Claim thread and closure

- Problem → position-resolved recorder diagnostics can inherit schedule geometry.

- Boundary → the evidence supports a recorder-level statement, not a model mechanism or cross-model/schedule/recorder law.
