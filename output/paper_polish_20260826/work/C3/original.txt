011                                               A BSTRACT
012
013
               We publish the reporting methodology of a frozen, closed repeated-query serving
               study as a positive methodological asset rather than concealing or overstating its
014
               evidence ceiling. The note contributes three instruments: the exact publishable
015
               scope of every surviving claim, a NOT_MEASURED register that is complete
016            relative to the claim ledger the authors enumerate (a declarative universe, not
017            an independent ground truth — see the limitation in Section 4), and the frozen
018            values carrying per-row scope labels. Two blockers previously read as hardware
019            boundaries turn out to be application-level defects in our own measurement code:
020            a thread-local grad-mode leak in the full-context worker (≈2 lines, now fixed)
021            and an argument mismatch in a vendored KV-selector (≈6–10 lines, statically
022            diagnosed only). A decisive concurrent fix-and-rerun (D1) confirms at L=32k
023            that the guarded full-context baseline is feasible — 29.31 GB peak at concurrency
024
               G=1, finite decode, no out-of-memory failure — where the unguarded code had
               aborted out of memory. The corresponding 16k figure, a measured pre-guard peak
025
               of 139.2 GB, is attributed to the same missing guard by inspection and remains
026
               NOT_MEASURED, because no 16k concurrent re-run was produced. The KV-
027            selector and adapter-matched arms remain NOT_MEASURED, so the earlier
028            “infeasible environment” reading is withdrawn rather than replaced by a positive
029            serving claim.

032   1    I NTRODUCTION AND S COPE
033
034
      An archived study of multi-query KV-selective serving was withdrawn after review identified an
      evidence ceiling rather than a writing defect: load-bearing claims could not be honestly measured
035
      on a worker whose GPU allocation was exhausted, whose declared adapter was unrecoverable, and
036
      whose environment aborted concurrent serving. Rather than bury the study, this note publishes the
037   reporting apparatus the failure produced and treats that apparatus as the contribution.
038
039   Contributions. Figure 1 shows how the three instruments compose. (a) Adjudicated scope state-
040
      ments for each surviving claim (Section 3), each with a re-runnable, pattern-scoped check. (b) A
      NOT_MEASURED register (Section 4) whose universe is the author-enumerated claim ledger —
041
      complete relative to that declared universe, without proving no claim was ever omitted. (c) Frozen
042
      values with per-row scope labels (concurrency, estimand class, status); a label documents how the
043   value may be cited but does not constrain a downstream author (Section 5). (d) A documented
044   withdrawal: the apparent memory ceiling was an application-level guard bug, confirmed by a matched
045   fix-and-rerun at one length and left unconfirmed at the other (Section 6). Everything is scoped to a
046   single GPU (H20-3e, 141 GB (Yang et al., 2025), Qwen3-8B bfloat16, SDPA, adapter-free unless
047   stated); multi-GPU, multi-day, or 1M-token concurrent serving is out of scope.
048
      What this note does NOT do. It does not resurrect the archived cost–quality conclusion, claim any
049   concurrent throughput/latency result, or claim adapter-arm identity is verified; these are register rows
050   and a binding consumption discipline (Section 7). The archived study used short internal identifiers;
051   Table 1 defines the ones the argument needs and Table 8 (Appendix E) the full ledger, so the note
052   reads from the PDF alone.

091   Measurement and reporting methodology. The closest prior work is not a serving system but
092   the literature on how empirical claims should be measured and reported. Systems benchmarking
093   names how performance results mislead (Hoefler & Belli, 2015) and how bias can invert a conclusion
094   drawn from careful experiments (Mytkowicz et al., 2009) — our failure mode, since the “capacity
095
      boundary” was a property of the measurement surface. Machine learning has adopted budget-aware
      reporting (Dodge et al., 2019), reproducibility checklists (Pineau et al., 2021), artifact evaluation
096
      (Krishnamurthi & Vitek, 2015), and structured documentation templates (Gebru et al., 2021; Mitchell
097
      et al., 2019). Two strands bear on a withdrawal note: pre-registration, which fixes analysis scope
098   before the result is known (Nosek et al., 2018), and non-replication reporting (Ferrari Dacrema et al.,
099   2019; Kapoor & Narayanan, 2023). Closest in spirit, assurance-case notation (the Goal Structuring
100   Notation; Kelly & Weaver, 2004) already makes undeveloped goals — claims an argument states but
101   does not yet establish — explicit in a structured tree. That idea is not new.
102
      What a NOT_MEASURED register adds over a Limitations section. Given that making unestab-
103
      lished claims explicit predates this work (GSN (Kelly & Weaver, 2004)), the register is a narrow,
104   machine-checked instance of that idea, not a new argument formalism. A Limitations section is
105   prose, author-selected, and unfalsifiable — nothing fails if a limitation is dropped; datasheets and
106   model cards fix the template, checklists fix the questions. The register adds three machine-checkable
107   properties (Section 4, Table 3, Section 3.1): a complete inventory relative to a declared universe,

      per-row binding labels (concurrency, estimand class, status) that forbid re-citing a value outside the
109   scope that produced it, and a machine-checked scope predicate a reviewer runs, exiting non-zero on
110   violation.

      Technical background for the withdrawn study. For context only, and contributing nothing here:
      the archived study sat among IO-aware exact attention (Dao et al., 2022), token- and page-level
113
      KV-cache management (Zhang et al., 2023; Xiao et al., 2024; Adnan et al., 2024; Kwon et al., 2023;

      3.1   S ERIAL - LEDGER DECOUPLING IS SCOPED , AND THE SCOPE IS MACHINE - CHECKED
125   The same-term cancellation in the archived cost accounting applies to the serial ledger only, and
126   we make zero wall-clock or concurrency-cancellation claim from that scoping. The only con-
127   current wall-clock ever measured on this stack (full-context, no adapter and no selector; Sec-
128   tion 6) carries no selection-cost S term, so there is no concurrent critical path on which an
129   S-cancellation could be asserted; the S-removal is licensed only as a serial-ledger same-term
130   cancellation (S0 =Sc on both serial-ledger sides). A reviewer re-runs the enumerated scope au-
131   dit python3 bundle/scanners/audit_p0_1_critical_path_scope.py --note
132   bundle/note/main.tex (0 GPU, standard library only); it exits 0 only when all four gates hold.
133
      The predicates, as implemented post-D1, with their arm restrictions:
134          • G1 (required).         An explicit serial-ledger-only scoping statement is present,
135            matched on the literal tokens serial-ledger, serial ledger, or same-term
136            cancellation.
137          • G2 (required, arm-restricted). A concurrent-serving NOT_MEASURED declaration is
138            present, matched on not_measured, not measured, or unmeasured. Post-D1 that
139            declaration is arm-restricted: it governs the KV-selector (j0) arm, the adapter-matched arm
140            and the 16k full-context arm, and not the full-context arm at L=32k, which Table 2 declares
141
               MEASURED. The gate checks such a declaration exists, not that every arm is unmeasured,
               so the two statements are consistent.
142
             • G3 (required). The withdrawn reading is not re-asserted: four phrase patterns are banned
143
               in non-negated sentences, including permanent (memory|capacity|hardware)
144            bound and verified in code. The script’s original docstring also required the re-
145            attribution to be called suspected; that gloss is superseded because D1 measured it at 32k.
146            The implemented predicate is unchanged.
147          • G4 (banned). No concurrent-critical-path S-cancellation claim and no concurrency-
148            throughput reading appears, matched against a published enumeration of eleven phrase
149            patterns in non-negated sentences.
150
      The gate has two published limits. First, it enumerates phrasings: an earlier revision passed
151
      G4 while a throughput-style reading sat in prose outside the enumeration; that reading was
152   removed, the enumeration extended, and the failure kept as a runnable red-team fixture
153   (bundle/scanners/redteam/) so a reviewer confirms the gate now rejects it. Second, verba-
154   tim code spans are exempt from matching — which is what lets this section print the banned literals
155   as specification, not assertion; prose is never exempt, so no enumerated place within the declared
156   phrasing universe remains to hide a claim. A reviewer who finds another uncovered phrasing has
157   falsified the gate’s coverage, not merely disagreed. Bounded by its enumeration, the gate is falsifiable
158   exactly because it is.

196   D-numbered decisive-evidence debts, and S-numbered soundness-deficit classes. Completeness is

198   falsified by exhibiting a ledger item with no row. The limit is that the ledger is author-constructed: the

202   after it belongs to that same ID (a swap that moves one ID’s meaning onto another’s row is caught
203   by the shipped mutation fixture), but a substitution borrowing no canonical clue, or one outside the
204
      per-ID text window, is unseen. A new failing mutation falsifies one of these bounds, as the red-team
      fixtures do.
