                                                  A BSTRACT
013
014
               Multiple-choice accuracy for a diffusion language model is not determined by the
015
               model alone: the scorer chooses which option tokens are visible, when they are
016            revealed, and whether revealed ground-truth tokens are fed back into later steps.
017            We audit that measurement construction on one bounded bed: LLaDA-8B-Instruct
018            with balanced four-choice GSM8K-MC.
019            On a conﬁrmatory n=400 pool s_conf exceeds the non-injected mc_uniform
020            reference by +13.0 pp at the per-candidate nmc,i = 2Li total-NFE-fair equaliza-
021            tion point (b/c = 109/57, exact p = 6.6×10−5 ; four tested budgets, conservative
022            read +9.0 pp). This refutes the narrow account that the advantage is an artifact
023            of the reference receiving less compute, but establishes no budget-independent
024            law. On a disjoint fresh pool a ﬁxed left-to-right order whose schedule selection
025            reads no model output still beats s_conf; conﬁdence ordering is therefore nei-
026
               ther necessary nor uniquely optimal on this answer format. Rank-agreement and
               distractor-perturbation diagnostics support only a bed-local, sign-consistent non-
027
               interchangeability reading; we do not claim dose-response, cross-bed transfer, or
028
               isolated causal attribution to feedback.

031   1    I NTRODUCTION
032
033   Masked diffusion language models assign a multiple-choice answer only after a scoring protocol
034   turns option-level denoising losses into a ranking. That protocol is part of the measurement instru-
035   ment. In particular, a scorer may reveal candidate tokens sequentially and feed the known candidate
036   token back into the context, or it may score a randomly masked subset without such feedback. Even
037   two feedback-based scorers can use different reveal schedules. Treating their accuracies as if they
038   were readings from the same ruler hides a change in what was measured.
039   We report a deliberately narrow audit of that construction. The evidence is one LLaDA-8B-Instruct
040   × GSM8K-MC four-option construction evaluated on two historical balanced pilot pools, a conﬁr-
041   matory 400-question pool for the reference-arm contrast, and a disjoint fresh 819-question pool for
042   reveal order. The study makes three claims, each tied to its pool and boundary (Table 3, Table 6).
043   First, on the tested n=400 reference-budget curve, s_conf’s advantage over the non-injected ref-
044   erence is positive and signiﬁcant at every tested budget and narrows as the reference is given more
045
      compute (Table 3, Figure 2). The registered matched-L point remains the primary endpoint and is
      measured on that bed; the per-candidate total-NFE-fair equalization at 2L (the decisive read, both
046
      arms 2Li forwards) is measured on the same bed and, within this n=400 endpoint change, reports
047
      the same headline +13.0 pp gap (exact p = 4.8×10−5 and 6.6×10−5 ); under the paper’s refusals
048   this does not license an estimand-external claim that endpoint choice is irrelevant in general, only
049   that on this tested bed the swap leaves the headline unchanged. The highest-budget read remains the
050   conservative one. This refutes a less-reference-compute-only account, not a budget-independent one,
051   and it is a complete protocol swap, not causal isolation. Second, reveal order on injected prompts,
052   measured against the tested persisted schedule, has a surviving effect on the disjoint fresh pool under
053   its k=1 multiplicity family; but a trivial ﬁxed order whose schedule selection reads no model output
      still beats s_conf on that pool on average (Table 6) — a margin that strengthens in the answer-pool

127   distinct values drawn from a frozen GSM8K answer pool. Every arm reuses the recorded choice set.
128   Historical and n = 400 pools are exactly answer-position balanced; the fresh pool is approximate.
129   Fresh-pool contrasts therefore mix protocol effects with construction stratum, and post-hoc position
130   standardization leaves the fresh–pilot difference, so we report them as strata-confounded. Inference
131   uses BF16, no temperature, and no generation. Conﬁdence and ﬁxed schedules are deterministic; the
132   seeded-random result is one persisted schedule. Because its runner used an unpinned Python string

      Fresh-pool recipe. The n = 819 study uses four-option GSM8K multiple-choice items drawn
651   without question overlap with the n = 400 pool; unlike the exactly balanced n = 400 pool, its
652   answer positions are only approximately balanced. All arms share the same model checkpoint,
653   prompts, item order, answer extractor, and recorded choice sets. The seeded-random arm is bound
654   to its persisted per-candidate orders because the original runner’s nominal seed derivation included
655   an unpinned Python string hash. The paired record contains one row per question and arm with the
656   prediction, reference answer, correctness, candidate-score vector, and order trace. The anonymous
