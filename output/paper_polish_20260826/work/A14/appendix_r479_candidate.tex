\section{Boundary Analysis and Registered Verdicts}
\label{sec:boundary}

\paragraph{Fresh-pool recipe.}
The $n=819$ study uses balanced four-option GSM8K multiple-choice items drawn
without question overlap with the $n=400$ pool.  All arms share the same model
checkpoint, prompts, item order, answer extractor, and item-keyed master seed.
Arm-specific counters branch deterministically from that seed.  The paired
record contains one row per question and arm with the prediction, reference
answer, correctness, and candidate-score vector.  The anonymous artifact
bundle additionally contains the preregistration, execution environment,
verdict, and heterogeneity recomputation described in
Appendix~\ref{app:repro}.

\section{Power and Sample-Size Ledger}
\label{app:powerledger}
The primary $n=400$ draw-matched contrast clears the registered Holm threshold
and its planning magnitude.  The historical $n=64$ draw-matched result is
detected in that sample with a slim margin over its detectable magnitude.  The
$n_{\rm mc}=128$ historical arm is underpowered and remains
\textsc{undetermined}, not zero.  Rank agreement and fixed 25\% perturbation
overlays are descriptive diagnostics rather than hypothesis tests.
