011                                                   Abstract
012
013              This reproducible pilot case study examines a negative control on one 135M-
014              parameter masked diffusion language model bed. It is confined to artifact reproduc-
015              tion and engineering diagnosis in this bed, and claims no new evaluation metric,
016
                 training method, or discovery. Under a matched short budget, the last-logged loss
                 favors autoregressive over random initialization by 2.26 nats, in the same direction
017
                 across four paired data-order seeds; the random initialization itself is one fixed
018
                 draw, so these repeats do not measure weight-draw variance. In a single-prompt
019              generation pilot both arms are visibly degenerate, yet teacher perplexity ranks them
020              opposite to the paired loss benchmark while effective length, alphabetic yield, and
021              emitted- token concentration separate their emissions. Without an independent
022              quality anchor, neither observation establishes metric capability or a quality rank-
023              ing. MAUVE is not recorded under this pilot configuration, so we form no ra-
024              tio and draw no MAUVE capability or quality inference. The engineering output
025              is instead a companion record for this pilot: place effective emission length and
026              emitted-token degeneration beside per-token scores. It is not a new scalar metric,
027
                 and nothing here extrapolates beyond the stated bed and pilot.
028

      1     Introduction
030
031   Masked diffusion language models generate by repeatedly denoising a canvas of masked tokens rather
032   than extending a causal prefix one token at a time (Sahoo et al., 2024; Nie et al., 2025). This design
033   exposes intermediate signals—entropy, maximum probability, mask state, and changes in the current
034   argmax—that can guide adaptive decoding (Li et al., 2025). Generated-text PPL, MAUVE, and
035   trajectory summaries describe different projections of an emission; none is an independent human-
036   quality anchor in our experiment. Wang et al. diagnose length, repetition, and punctuation as factors
037   that can distort PLM-based perplexity (Wang et al., 2022). We therefore ask only whether two known-
038
      degenerate outputs in one small dLLM pilot differ on analogous emission fields that our initial report
      did not place beside each score.
039
040   This paper turns a small training bed into a controlled negative-control pilot case study. The bed
041   starts from SmolLM2-135M (Ben Allal et al., 2025) and converts the same architecture to a masked
042
      denoiser. One arm reuses autoregressive weights; the other uses a fixed random-initialization check-
      point. Data, optimizer, schedule, token count, hardware, sampler, and evaluation are held constant.
043
      This design does not estimate performance over the space of random initializations. It asks a nar-
044
      rower question: at a fixed 30.0M-token budget, how do the two degenerate arms differ on directly
045   audited emission fields when the initialization is replaced by a constructive negative control?
046
      Three categories are easy to conflate. Reproduction means that the reported arithmetic and artifacts
047
      can be rerun; it is not a multisite replication. Engineering diagnosis means applying a transparent
048
      audit to this bed. A genuinely new method would require an independently validated metric, training
049   rule, or decoder. This paper is a reproducible negative-control diagnosis in the first two categories,
050   not the third. Its three concrete deliverables are:
051
052           1. A matched-budget calibration shows a same-signed loss separation over four paired data-
053              order seeds: the mean last-logged loss gap is 2.262, with 95% CI [2.220, 2.305]. The result is
                 explicitly conditional on the two fixed initialization checkpoints and short 458-step horizon.

092   2   Related Work and Evaluation Framing
093
094   Masked diffusion language modeling. Masked diffusion objectives can be written as mixtures of
095   masked-language modeling losses and paired with iterative unmasking samplers (Sahoo et al., 2024).
096   LLaDA demonstrated that the paradigm scales to an 8B parameter model trained from scratch (Nie
097   et al., 2025); more recent systems span reusable training frameworks (Zhou et al., 2026), continuous
098
      embedded flows (Hu et al., 2026), and adaptive generation length (Li et al., 2025). AR-to-diffusion
      adaptation has already been studied systematically in the DiffuLLaMA family (Gong et al., 2025);
099
      PreDiff-LM studies pretrained masked diffusion with hybrid attention (Yao et al., 2026), and UNIFU-
100
      SION studies AR adaptation under a unified reverse-rate objective (Jiang et al., 2026). Accordingly,
101   our matched initialization arms are a small reproducible case study, not a new adaptation method or
102   training discovery.
103
104   Metrics score different projections of emitted text. Generated-text PPL scores teacher likeli-
105   hood; Wang et al. show that length, repetition, and punctuation can distort that score (Wang et al.,
106   2022). MAUVE instead compares generated and human distributions (Pillutla et al., 2021); surface
107   form also matters. Final-argmax share describes concentration of the generator’s decisions. Within
      the 11-source local verification ledger used for this paper, we did not establish that these sources

145   3.2    Matched arms and training budget
146
147   Both arms use the converted SmolLM2-135M architecture (Ben Allal et al., 2025; Zhou et al., 2026).
148
      The AR-init arm retains pretrained weights; random-init is the fixed, audited from-scratch draw in
      Table 2. Crucially, the four paired repeats vary data order and training stochasticity, not this weight
149
      draw, so their loss variance cannot be transferred to independently drawn random initializations. To
150
      test that gap we ran a pre-registered multi-init negative control (Table 3) on three independently
151   drawn random-initialization checkpoints, each trained under the same 30M-token / seed-42 data or-
152   dering as the two principal arms (Section 5). On the loss side the negative control reproduces: the
153   paired last-logged-loss gap (random − initR − AR − init) is positive on all three draws (bootstrap
154   95% CI lower bound > 0), so the sign-consistency of the principal result does not collapse across
155   random initializations. The emission-side degeneration mode, however, is initialization-dependent:
156   two draws degrade into a repeated-word lexical loop while the third is a shorter “gray” profile, so
157   the emission narrative does not transfer across draws and is scoped to this effect (Section 5; evidence
158   hash and verified fields are in paper/anonymous_artifact/data/reproducibility_
159
      anchors.json).
160   All token accounting, runtime, logging, objective, scheduler, hardware, and provenance fields are
161   frozen in Table 2; in particular, “final” means the step-450 log inside the 458-step run, not a separate
      step-458 evaluation.

232   3.3                         Generation and metric protocol
233
234   Table 2 gives the complete generation grid. It is a single-checkpoint, single-prompt, 32-sample-per-
235   cell pilot rather than a multi-seed quality estimate. As preregistered, we report median causal PPL
236   under GPT-2 and SmolLM2; the median prevents two catastrophic AR-init outliers at τ = 0.7 from
237
      dominating. SmolLM2 is weight-related to AR-init and may share FineWeb-Edu source-domain bias
      with this experiment, so agreement with GPT-2 mitigates but does not remove teacher dependence.
238
239   Each PPL cell is accompanied by emitted length, alphabetic-word count, and a pooled corpus top-
240   word share. The share is an illustrative cell aggregate; the artifact does not contain a per-continuation
241
      concentration distribution, so we make no continuation-level concentration claim. These fields de-
      scribe the scored emission rather than its quality. The attempted MAUVE value and the final-step
242
      argmax diagnostic are retained under the same rule: both remain routine raw records, but neither is
243
      used for a pilot quality or capability inference. MAUVE is not recorded under Table 2, and strict
244   top-1 remains separate from sampled-token frequency at nonzero temperature.
245
246   3.4                         Frozen signal layer and probes
247
248   Schema v3.1 records mask state, entropy, maximum probability, top-k identities and probabilities,
249   commit step, final argmax, and masked-position drift with explicit dtypes and shapes; evaluation
250
      labels remain outside the trajectory layer. One dump therefore supports unigram concentration, tie-
      aware unresolved-position drift, and committed-position sweep-tail analyses without another model
251
      run.
252
253   A fourth analysis applies one instrument to both 135M arms: among currently masked positions,
254
      entropy is scored against whether the current argmax differs from the eventual final token. Table 10
      reports the raw AUROC, trajectory-bootstrap interval (B = 2,000, seed 0), trajectory and step counts,
255
      row support, and permutation calibration (Monte Carlo B = 400, seed 0). The calibration estimand
256
      is real/null95, where null95 is the 95th percentile of the permutation null; a value above one supplies
257   at most a weak orientation check, not a quality label.

266   statistics vary data order and training randomness conditional on fixed initialization checkpoints, not

366   4.3                                   Trajectory records under a failed drift recomputation gate
367
368   The probes give three complementary views. First, random-init has the more concentrated final-step
369   argmax (Table 8). Second, the original claim that low-confidence layers cause drift is unsupported:
370   the failed scalar/top-k gate leaves no quantitative error bound for drift localization. The passing tie-
371   bound and decoding-alignment checks establish only that the serialization is internally bounded and
372   aligned; they do not recover the failed counts or a directional mechanism. Third, AR-init falls from
373   high entropy to a mid-trajectory minimum and rises to a high-entropy residue, whereas random-init
374
      stays nearly flat (Figure 3). This stored-entropy contrast does not use the failed drift field, but the
      nearly untrained control (458 steps, final loss 7.36) still cannot distinguish learned structure from the
375

406   5     Discussion
407
408   A negative control is a reporting unit test. The random-initialized arm is a unit test for describing
409   distinct degeneration modes, not a competitive baseline. For this single 458-step, single-checkpoint
410   pilot, the within-arm check in Section 4.2 supports only this companion bundle:
411                            (median teacher PPL, distributional similarity, E),                        (1)
412
      where E is effective length, alphabetic yield, and emitted-token concentration. The attempted
413
      MAUVE value and argmax diagnostic follow the same retention standard: raw records remain, but
414
      neither supports inference. Slot (ii) is therefore unexercised; argmax is not an emission slot because
415   it decouples from sampled emissions here (Section 4.2, Figure 1). These fields are not a new metric,
416   and the multi-init control supports only the loss-side sign, not the PPL/loss ordering.
417
418   Loss calibration and generation quality answer different questions. The paired loss gap is sta-
419   tistically repeated; generation uses only seed-42 checkpoints and diagnoses emission coordinates,
420   not production quality. Neither implies good text, a MAUVE gap, or a quality ranking for AR-init.
421
422   The signal layer is infrastructure, not a new decoder. The schema separates masked from com-
423   mitted positions, current argmax from held token, and top-k ties. It diagnoses this control; adaptive-
424   decoder accuracy or efficiency remains outside the evidence.

      7      Conclusion
456
      In this 135M negative-control pilot, autoregressive initialization retains a 2.262-nat last-logged loss
457   advantage across four paired data orders, while one seed-42 generation check contrasts two degener-
458   ate emissions. Effective length, content yield, and concentration distinguish them; PPL is recorded
459   but the pinned MAUVE attempt is not. Without an independent quality anchor, this is only a compan-
460   ion record for this setting—not a quality ordering, metric capability, new metric, training discovery,
461   or extrapolation to other dLLMs.
