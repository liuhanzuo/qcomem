                                                  A BSTRACT
013
014            Exact 2:4 sparsity couples support decisions locally, while post-training recovery
015            can obscure their downstream differences. We examine three registered fixed
016            supports—ALPS, ELSA-4096, and ProxSparse—after the same 625M-token joint
017            sparse-weight and rank-16 SLoRB recovery. Their cross-support AVG-9 point-
018            estimate range contracts from 2.675 pp before recovery to 0.118 pp afterward. The
019            latter is smaller than the harness’s 0.52 pp marginal binomial standard error for
020            a single checkpoint, but that quantity is not a pairwise or joint resolution limit.
021            Per-item predictions were not retained, so the joint uncertainty of the post-recovery
022
               range and residual support differences are unidentified. The result is therefore an
               archived descriptive observation and a measurement caution: claims at this scale
023
               require paired per-item analysis. The curvature score, annealing schedule, and
024
               SLoRB branch remain pipeline background because they were not isolated under
025            matched budgets. An archived 5B checkpoint provides supporting context only: it
026            is evaluated with SLoRB active, has no folded exact-2:4 export or pre/post-export
027            comparison, and its recovery-corpus manifest is unavailable.

030   1   I NTRODUCTION
031
032
      Semi-structured sparsity is one of the few compression regimes that combines substantial parameter
      removal with native accelerator support. In particular, NVIDIA sparse Tensor Cores execute exact
033
      2:4 patterns, retaining two weights in every group of four (NVIDIA, 2020). The same constraint that
034
      makes deployment efficient also couples pruning decisions: each candidate competes locally, and a
035   mistaken hard selection cannot be repaired without changing the support.
036
037   We ask a narrower empirical question: if several native supports undergo an identical token budget and
038
      recovery recipe, does their cross-support spread persist? The comparisons summarized in Section 2 do
      not isolate this before/after quantity across multiple labelled supports. Our load-bearing contribution
039
      is the archived measurement of that quantity; the SparseForge pipeline below is background context,
040
      not a validated mechanism.
041
042   Calibration-only methods such as Wanda and SparseGPT (Sun et al., 2023; Frantar & Alistarh, 2023)
043   are inexpensive, while retraining-based AST and CAST (Huang et al., 2024; 2025) recover more
044
      quality at larger token budgets. This leaves a practical question: can a recovery run spend its tokens
      on improving the structured support itself, rather than merely adapting weights to an early hard mask?
045
046   As pipeline background, S PARSE F ORGE (Figure 1) maintains a continuous mask and alternates
047   task/distillation updates of the sparse base weights and rank-16 SLoRB branch with periodic curvature-
048   score mask updates. A soft-to-hard schedule then moves the mask toward an exact 2:4 pattern. This
049   describes the archived training pipeline rather than an isolated causal mechanism: the reported
050
      checkpoint keeps the SLoRB branch live, and folding it into a deployable exact-2:4 export is a
      registered future step (Appendix C), not an executed result. These three components have never been
051
      isolated under matched budgets; none of the reported gains can be attributed to any single mechanism.
052

      ports span 2.675 pp in AVG-9 before recovery and 0.118 pp after the same 625M-token recov-

093   2    R ELATED W ORK
094
095   One-shot and calibration-scale pruning. Magnitude pruning and second-order criteria established
096
      the basic importance-estimation view (Han et al., 2015; LeCun et al., 1990; Hassibi & Stork, 1993).
      For LLMs, SparseGPT uses approximate second-order information and Wanda combines weights
097
      with activation statistics (Frantar & Alistarh, 2023; Sun et al., 2023). ALPS performs layerwise
098
      constrained reconstruction (Meng et al., 2024); ELSA and ProxSparse optimize semi-structured
099   supports or parameters at calibration scale (Lee et al., 2026; Liu et al., 2025). These approaches avoid
100   a large recovery corpus, but their support decisions remain consequential under exact local structure.
101
102   Semi-structured sparse training. MaskLLM learns discrete N :M masks with a large corpus, so
103   its mask is trained rather than frozen from a calibration decision (Fang et al., 2024). AST anneals
104   dense weights toward 2:4 and uses SLoRB for recovery (Huang et al., 2024); CAST uses a continuous
105   differentiable relaxation (Huang et al., 2025). These approaches motivate learned structure, but
106   the comparisons available to this study do not fix and label several native supports, apply the same
107   recovery recipe to each, and measure the resulting cross-support spread. Our contribution is that
      archived measurement rather than a novel algorithmic advantage. Because the SparseForge mask

      3     P IPELINE R ATIONALE (BACKGROUND )
113
114
      In a group of four weights, one incorrect survivor necessarily excludes another candidate. A one-shot
      score can therefore select a poor subspace before recovery begins. Keeping the mask soft allows
115
      repeated evidence to reverse early rankings, but a soft gate is not deployable. The central problem
116
      is consequently a constrained transition: preserve exploration early, inject exact group structure
117   progressively, and minimize the final soft-to-hard projection gap.
118
119   Curvature gives a local survival signal. For a weight wi near a pretrained solution,
120                                                   ∂L       1 ∂2L 2
                                          ∆Li ≈ −        wi +          w .                                 (1)
121                                                  ∂wi       2 ∂wi2 i
122   When the first-order term is small, magnitude alone misses the loss curvature associated with deletion.
123   Optimal Brain Damage motivates second-order importance via a diagonal Hessian proxy (LeCun
124   et al., 1990), while Optimal Brain Surgeon and WoodFisher go beyond the diagonal precisely because
125   cross-term and inverse-curvature effects matter under exact structure (Hassibi & Stork, 1993; Singh
126   & Alistarh, 2020): for N :M groups the deleting mask couples coordinates within a group, so the
127   diagonal term is only a first-order faithful ranking signal, not a certificate. S PARSE F ORGE therefore
128   uses the diagonal curvature score to rank candidates within each structured group (the cheap sufficient
129
      signal for which column of a 2:4 group to drop) and makes no stronger approximation claim than
      OBD; annealing, not the curvature proxy alone, is what introduces the final support gradually.
130
      Throughout this paper we use “match” in the sense of numerical equivalence within stated sampling
131
      error, not of verified behavioral equivalence between checkpoints; per-item agreement between
132   SparseForge and AST has not been established in this workspace.

      The narrow post-recovery spread among ALPS-, ELSA-, and ProxSparse-supported endpoints shows

347   6     L IMITATIONS
348
349          • Endpoint and uncertainty scope. The recovery-compression result uses three archived
350            endpoints rather than a contemporary multi-seed study. The 0.52 pp quantity is a marginal
351            per-checkpoint binomial standard error, not pairwise or joint uncertainty or training-run
352            variance; per-item predictions were not retained. Historical error bars use different legacy
353            suites and do not transfer to the current harness (Appendix E).
354          • Budget and mechanism scope. No valid 625M-token matched-budget S PARSE F ORGE
355            endpoint exists, so the archived 5B comparison is not a token-normalized sample-efficiency
356            result. The curvature score, annealing schedule, and SLoRB branch were not isolated
357            under matched budgets, and no reported gain is attributable to one component. Curvature
358            estimation and retraining also cost more than one-shot pruning.
359          • Export and provenance scope. Every reported SparseForge number is a branch-active
360            training-state measurement. The SLoRB fold, exact 2:4 reprojection, group-sum assertion,
361            and pre/post-export quality remain unimplemented or unmeasured (Appendix C, EXT-M3).
362            The archived weights and corpus manifest are unreachable, so neither export equivalence
363
               nor corpus attribution can be resolved within this study.
364          • External validity. Cross-family results are archived single runs without recoverable per-seed
365            or complete harness metadata. The long-budget ELSA-support recipe diverges and must be
366            redesigned before supporting a method-level conclusion. Historical H800/TensorRT-LLM
367
               measurements come from an unverifiable earlier export, so they do not establish speed for
               the checkpoint studied here or transfer across hardware and workloads.

765   C.4   T IE RULE AND EXACT-2:4 VALIDATION
766
767   Every structured selection uses the indices returned by torch.topk(..., largest=True)
768   and creates the binary target by scatter_. This guarantees two written ones per group for a returned
769   index tuple, including the structured_exact path, but the frozen code adds no deterministic
770   secondary key. PyTorch/CUDA does not expose a stable mathematical ordering for equal scores, so
771   tied groups have an underspecified support: the kernel’s returned indices decide the survivors, and a
772   unique cross-device tie rule is not guaranteed.
773   The required export unit assertion is therefore the groupwise, not merely global, check
774
775   assert torch.all(hard.view(d_out,d_in//4,4).sum(-1) == 2)
776
777   At the frozen commit, legacy/check_sparsity.py:57-121,137-181 checks bi-
778   nary/global mask sparsity and mask–weight zero overlap, but contains no group-sum-= 2 assertion.
779   It cannot honestly be cited as that exact unit test until the assertion above is added to the export
780
      validator. During training, nm_2_4_tile_stats (legacy/utils.py:1029-1234) reports
      top-2/bottom-2 mask means and soft–hard gaps; these are useful trajectory diagnostics but do not
781
      replace the exact export assertion.
