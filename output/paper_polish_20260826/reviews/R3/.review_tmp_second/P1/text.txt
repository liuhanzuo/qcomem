                                                 A BSTRACT
013
014
               Multi-agent research runs leave fire archives: pre-registrations, endpoint code,
015
               and reports that claim a decision fired. We present AUDIT-GATE, a static zero-
016
               GPU audit developed during one live run, together with a transparency record of
017            what was pre-registered, amended after observation, preserved, or found to have
018            drifted. The audit returns exactly one of PASS, FAIL, or POSTPONE. Each verdict
019            includes a replayable certificate consisting of pinned inputs, a pinned checker,
020            and one command. This bundle is an interface that a third party can execute; no
021            third-party execution is claimed here.
022            AUDIT-GATE combines four archive checks—endpoint reachability, boxed posi-
023            tive controls, manifest acceptance, and unreachable-primary-leg archiving—with a
024            five-field endpoint registration rule, a healthy-arm non-degeneracy criterion, and
025            PRIM5, which projects raw machine strings onto the three verdict states. The
026            field audit comprises a six-case frozen-checker ledger and a preregistered decisive
027
               cell over seven archives from other seats in the same run. At the frozen auditor-
               transcription tier, the decisive cell contains 6 PASS, 1 FAIL, and 0 POSTPONE;
028
               after mechanical extraction and current-verdict synchronization, it contains 3 PASS,
029
               2 FAIL, and 2 POSTPONE.
030
               The fourteen frozen field units exercise PASS and FAIL but not POSTPONE. Eight
031
               synthetic defect-injected archives exercise the missing state and every emitting
032            clause; the sole zero is the intended absence of an unregistered verdict string.
033            These fixtures establish mechanical branch coverage, not detection or false-alarm
034            performance on natural archives. Because the run contains no independently
035            labelled archive population, we report no such rates and instead register the design
036            needed to measure them. AUDIT-GATE tests whether a verdict is mechanically
037            operable and replayable, not whether it is scientifically correct.
038
039
040   1   I NTRODUCTION
041
042
      A fire claim states that a preregistered decision point fired. In a multi-agent run, the supporting
      archive contains a frozen preregistration, consumer scripts, and result reports. Three forms of
043
      drift complicate review: prose can diverge from artifacts, rerunning current code may no longer
044
      reproduce the registered computation, and producer self-attestation need not track the computation
045   performed (Turpin et al., 2023). AUDIT-GATE addresses the earlier, static question of whether the
046   registered decision is mechanically reachable and replayable. Figure 1 summarizes the resulting
047   object. Because the gate was built inside one live run, we report post-observation amendments as part
048   of its construction history (Tables 12 and 8).
049
      Many archives fail before a statistical question is reachable: no consumer script computes the
050
      registered readout; a rate is defined over an empty denominator; a manifest references missing,
051   unmarked artifacts; or a supposedly healthy zero comes from a deterministic replay of the same
052   path. Detecting these conditions requires neither a GPU nor a model, only a static inspection of the
053   recorded files.

082
      AUDIT-GATE maps a fire archive to PASS, FAIL, or POSTPONE using filesystem- and string-level
083
      facts. A verdict is reportable only with a replayable certificate: a frozen script that recomputes it from
084   record-time anchors. Our contributions are:
085
086         1. A meta-primitive layer (Section 2): four primitives PRIM1–PRIM4, the G1 endpoint quin-
087            tuple (readout, side, n, threshold, path_id), the G2 non-degenerate-healthy-arm criterion,
088            a disambiguation of when a consumption verdict is evaluated, and PRIM5, which closes the
089            verdict domain by pinning the projection from raw machine strings onto the three states.
090         2. A re-runnable certificate discipline: a verdict counts only if a frozen script reproduces
091            it and every anchor is pinned to its record-time location. The bundled pin, checker, and
092            one-command replay form an independent re-run interface that a third party can execute;
093            we do not claim that one already has. We separate record-time semantics from today’s
094            write-time re-pinning and disclose every disagreement.
095         3. A two-ledger field audit (Section 3): a six-case ledger over real in-run fire archives, plus a
096            decisive cell whose pre-registration was frozen before it ran and which replays the audit over
097            seven external archives, including one true detection and one deliberate non-kill. Because the
098            live corpus turned out to exercise only two of the three states, we add eight synthetic defect-
099            injected archives that exercise the third and close the anchor enumeration (Section 3.3), and
100
               we report the resulting coverage gap explicitly rather than describing the observed verdict
               set as non-vacuous.
101
102
      Two framing clauses hold throughout. Mechanical operability is not empirical soundness: a PASS
103   may still be statistically wrong, and we make no claim about an archive’s science. The verdict
104   map, PRIM3 enumeration, G2 predicate, and tier reading changed after observation; we disclose
105   these changes rather than pretending the current shape was pre-registered. Appendix H is the single
106   current-verdict statement.

      2     F RAMEWORK
109
110
      Objects. A fire archive A consists of a pre-registration, a set of consumer scripts C(A) that are
111   supposed to compute the registered reading, a manifest M (A) of referenced artifacts, and reports.
112   The audit target is not the reading but the verdict: can a second machine, given only A as it was
113   recorded, recompute the same three-state outcome? Figure 4 fixes the order in which the checks of
114   this section are applied and names the terminal verdict each one may emit.
115
116   2.1   G1: THE ENDPOINT QUINTUPLE
117
118   Every registered endpoint must be pinned as
119                               e = (readout, side, n, threshold, path_id),

      and a missing field returns POSTPONE with the minimal refill, never a bare “insufficient sam-
121
      ple”. Thresholds are meaningful only within one path_id; cross-path differences are outside
122
      that endpoint’s calibration. Appendix G gives the full path identity and the decisive cell’s per-unit
123   registrations.

      2.2   PRIM1–PRIM4
126
127   PRIM1 (reachability envelope). The endpoint side, readout, and n must be pinned. Let [L, U ]
128   be the two-sided 95% Clopper–Pearson interval (Clopper & Pearson, 1934) for k successes in n
129   trials at α = 0.05, and let θ be the registered threshold. An endpoint is design-decidable when at its
130   registered n some observable outcome either crosses θ or certifies equivalence, and observed-decided
131   when the observed interval actually does so:
132                LO : U ≤ θ;       HI : L ≥ θ;        ABS (TOST) : [L, U ] ⊆ [θ − δ, θ + δ]
133
      for a pre-registered margin δ > 0. The retired singleton-intersection reading [L, U ] ∩ {θ} trivialized
134
      at n = 1: it made every such endpoint automatically “decided.” Under TOST, n = 1 is never
135
      automatically observed-decided, and an unregistered δ is a post-hoc zone. An interval satisfying no
136   branch yields POSTPONE with a minimal-refill obligation. For the degenerate zero column k = 0,
137                                                          1/n
138                                         U (n) = 1 − α2          ,
139
140   so the refill is the integer n⋆ = min{ n : U (n) ≤ θ }. The exercised numeric instance and its checked
141   refill are reported in Table 11 (PTSD-2); the runner recomputes n⋆ by search rather than reciting it.
142   PRIM2 (boxed positive control). Every gate must carry at least one must-FAIL arm built from
143   a known-illegal input, boxed away from the primary data, with the acceptance criterion that the
144   must-FAIL arm fires on all of its cases while the anti-pass arm fires on none. A positive control that
145   passes unconditionally has zero evidential strength, and the audit reports FAIL rather than a passing
146   gate.
147
      PRIM3 (manifest three-arm acceptance). For every artifact referenced by a manifest, exactly
148   one of three arms applies: present with matching hash (PASS); present with drifted hash and no
149   recorded cause (FAIL); absent without an explicit ABSENT marker (FAIL). The third arm is the
150   one that produced this framework’s own constructive self-FAIL, case F3 of Section 3.1; we make
151   no claim that it is the most productive arm, because in our corpus no detecting arm fired more than
152   once (Figure 2). These three arms are the states the field corpus produced; the decision is really
153   a function of four record-time facts, and Section 3.3 closes the enumeration at five states, adding
154   drift-with-a-recorded-cause (PASS) and absent-with-a-marker (POSTPONE).
155   PRIM4 (unreachable primary leg). If the primary leg’s self-test corpus is unreachable in the round
156   being claimed — zero scorable rows, empty manifest — the negative-result archiving verdict holds
157   automatically, and re-labelling the failed primary run as a sensitivity analysis is forbidden.

167   2.4   W HERE THE CONSUMPTION VERDICT IS EVALUATED
168
169   A recurring ambiguity is when to judge that a primitive was consumed. We bind the evaluation to
170   the producer’s own pre-registered binding decision point, then to the later of (the consumer’s first
171   consuming round, the producer’s release round), plus K observer rounds with K = 2 by default.
172   This single rule excludes the two opposite readings that otherwise coexist: “any action by any seat
173
      counts as consumption” and “only the producing seat’s actions count”.
174
175   2.5   A DVISORY STATUS , DISCLOSED IN FULL
176
      The fan-in ledger that records consumption evidence carries the structural property cannot_FAIL_
177
      by_design = true, and we keep that disclosure in the main text rather than in a footnote. Its
178   meaning is narrow and must not be inflated in either direction: the ledger never auto-promotes itself
179   to a mandatory gate, which is not the same as never emitting FAIL. It does emit FAIL verdicts,
180   and the two on record are a constructive self-FAIL on this framework’s own ledger (four of five
181   recorded consumer paths absent at their recorded locations without ABSENT markers) and a reusable
182   counterfactual template any seat can apply post hoc. A scoreboard is not a gate; we report it as
183   advisory evidence, never as a blocking condition on another seat.
184
185   2.6   W HAT “ MECHANICALLY OPERABLE ” MEANS
186
187
      A verdict is mechanically operable when (a) it follows from filesystem- or string-level facts, (b) a
      re-runnable script certificate recomputes it, and (c) every anchor is pinned to the artifact’s location
188
      at the time of record. Condition (c) is not pedantry: an early version of our own audit resolved an
189
      anchor to where the file would be looked up later, and a failure to reproduce at that later path is a
190   stale anchor, not a falsified original verdict.
191
192   Freeze-discipline disclosure. The first-run implementation contradicted the registered diversity
193   predicate and returned FAIL_VACUOUS_STATES; it was corrected only after observing the cell.
194   Worse, that edition was replaced before it was pinned, so the verdict is recorded but not recomputable.
195   By our own PRIM3 and certificate rule this is a self-FAIL of our record-keeping, not a repairable
196   footnote. The verdict-state map, PRIM3 enumeration, and G2 predicate also changed after observation.
197   Table 12 and Appendix C preserve the version-by-version account, including the record-time/write-
198   time distinction.
199
200   2.7   PRIM5: VERDICT- DOMAIN CLOSURE
201
202
      Checkers emit reason-bearing machine strings, not states. PRIM5 therefore registers a map from every
      emitted string onto exactly one of PASS, FAIL, or POSTPONE and applies it by script. Provenance
203
      prefixes are stripped; an absent string becomes UNMAPPED and makes the certificate fail; unit and
204
      group strings occupy separate codomains; and each entry says how it was exercised. The complete
205   rules and map are in Appendix C; the projection command is in Appendix A.
206
207   What a group verdict does and does not assert. The decisive cell’s group verdict is about
208   audit execution, not a conjunction over archive verdicts: it says the audit ran, mapped every string,
209   exercised its controls, and separated at least two projected states. Its canonical machine string is
210   EXECUTION_VALID (renamed from AUDIT_EXECUTION_PASS so the label cannot be misread
211   as archive operability). Panel B of Table 1 separately reports archive operability and includes a FAIL;
212   Appendix C preserves the rule and its bidirectional disagreements.

389   External positioning: three literatures this is not. Provenance and experiment-lineage systems
390   record how values and models were produced (Cheney et al., 2009; Vartak et al., 2016; Schelter
391   et al., 2017; Zaharia et al., 2018), while versioning pins their artifacts (Kuprieiev et al., 2020);
392   AUDIT-GATE instead asks whether a claimed adjudication is recomputable. Reporting standards
393   and preregistration govern author-side disclosure (Pineau et al., 2021; Gebru et al., 2021; Forde &
394   Paganini, 2019), and production assertions inspect data or model behaviour in flight (Polyzotis et al.,
395   2019; Kang et al., 2020); our audit acts later on a released archive. Content-addressed deployment and
396
      reproducible builds establish byte identity (Dolstra et al., 2004; Lamb & Zacchiroli, 2022); PRIM3
      additionally distinguishes explained drift from absence because its target is verdict stability. If these
397
      deltas amount only to MLOps governance common sense, the registered MERGE clause requires
398
      folding the framework into a survey and retiring it.

401   5       FALSIFICATION AND SCOPE
402
403   The four registered exits remain. PASS is a population-level aspiration: all mechanically unreachable
404   endpoints are caught (false-open = 0) and no operable archive is killed (false-kill = 0); a finite
405
      corpus can certify only bounds on those rates. KILL fires if an independently confirmed unreachable
      endpoint is judged PASS or the false-kill rate is evidenced above 5%. MERGE withdraws the
406
      framework into a survey if it merely restates MLOps governance. STOP-PASS additionally requires
407
      one independent external fire claim to adopt the primitives as its pre-creation gate.
408
409   Two further candidates came from in-run review (Appendix F). Clause (vii) is falsified by a gate
410   that reaches its registered bound, has no post-hoc discriminating power, and is not rejected by (viii).
411
      Clause (viii) is falsified by byte-identical load-bearing fields that do not make a downstream paired or
      maximum comparison tautological.
412
413   Two scope limits follow. A PASS means recomputable, not scientifically correct. Nor do we calibrate
414   the audit’s false-alarm rate or power: the fixtures do not close this gap because they share an author
415   with their checkers. Calibration requires a labelled fire-archive population whose operability status is
416   established independently of us, which does not exist inside a live run.
417   Mitigation (i) is an author-withheld-label blind calibration over 8 frozen fixture labels: a blind wrapper
418   reproduced a diagonal confusion matrix and reports two-sided Clopper–Pearson bounds (analysis/
419   r83_i22_calib/I22_BLIND_CALIBRATION_R83.json); because the fixtures and checker
420   share an author, this is a mitigation, not calibration closure, and it is not third-party execution.
421   Mitigation (ii) is a clean-room bundle with single-entry replay.py, a hash-first-frozen MANIFEST,
422   and a self-test reporting replay_pass=true (experiments/bundle_i21_r83.tar.gz);
423
      it is a mitigation, not calibration closure, and it is not third-party execution. Together they make
      label visibility and replay packaging auditable while leaving the missing independent population and
424
      third-party run unresolved.
425
426   What is available is the design that would settle the question, registered before any number exists. The
427   v1 cell is superseded before execution; the v2 cell is blocked, not merely unrun: it needs at least 118
428   independently labelled defect-free archives, and unblocks only with a labeller outside the authoring
429
      seat, an outcome that remains human_pending. Table 3 records the decisive inequalities; analysis/
      D2_DECISION_REACHABILITY_R52.json verifies a total, single-valued outcome map.

449   The exit ledger remains unresolved. PASS is unmet because no independent-operability denominator

456   replay interface portable and self-checking, but no third party has executed it. Appendix A gives the
457   commands and Appendix H the attested pin.

1163   The six inherited anti-patterns. Reused, not reproved: (i) an empty set must fail a gate rather than
1164   pass it; (ii) a rate-only test is blind to direction; (iii) a run identifier must be a field, not a directory
1165   name; (iv) a truncated output is not a wrong answer; (v) short-sample behaviour does not certify
1166   long-generation behaviour; (vi) static inspection cannot see a runtime crash. PRIM2 and PRIM3 are
1167   the mechanical forms of (i) and (iii); (iv) and (v) survive as reasons why a readout-layer gate remains
1168   necessary alongside this one.

1172
1173   (vii) A structural reachability precheck is a sufficient-rejection, necessary-but-not-sufficient-admission
1174   gate: a pre-registered upper bound computed before ordering — for instance max(b + c) for a
1175   McNemar-framed test, or a permutation design’s pfloor together with a tolerance table — proves
1176   only that the test is not mechanically unanswerable. It does not prove that admission was correct. A
1177
       positive control that purports to demonstrate no false kill must itself survive clause (viii); otherwise
       its pass records a false admission.
1178
1179   (viii) Any reference arm or control fixture whose load-bearing numeric fields are byte-identical to
1180   another arm’s, beyond the metadata divergence one would expect, FAILs the audit. The converse
1181   trap is the reason the clause is written on fields rather than on files: two arms with distinct bytes and
1182   distinct inodes can still carry the same measurement — in the case that prompted this clause, a run’s
1183
       always and never reference pair agreed byte-for-byte in 20 of 22 top-level fields — so the audit
