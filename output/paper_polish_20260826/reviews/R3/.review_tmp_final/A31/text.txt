                                                 A BSTRACT
013
014
               Position-decomposed diagnostics of diffusion large language models can confuse
015
               sampler geometry with model behavior because an active-block recorder exposes
016
               positions only through the current sampling window. We hold the model, ques-
017            tions, canvas, decoding rule, and total function evaluations ﬁxed while applying
018            a composite intervention: block partition changes together with the required per-
019            block step allocation. On this bed, the detected boundary period follows block
020            length. Separately registered non-nested probes on a distinct canvas-96 bed do
021            not reveal a recorder-observable ﬁxed-32 component. We therefore propose a two-
022            layer reporting rule: state what boundary-set composition makes reachable before
023            observing data, then state what the measured magnitudes support. The composite
024            intervention, amended decision branch, and untested cross-model, cross-schedule,
025
               and cross-recorder scope preclude a model-mechanism claim.

      1   I NTRODUCTION
028
029
      Diffusion language models generate discrete sequences by iterative denoising (Austin et al., 2021;
030   Zheng et al., 2024; Lou et al., 2024; Shi et al., 2024; Nie et al., 2025). As decoding schedules
031   become variable or structured (Li et al., 2025; Sun et al., 2026; Wang et al., 2026), a position-
032   resolved diagnostic can inherit periodicity from the schedule that exposes its positions. Without an
033   intervention, such a readout cannot distinguish model-intrinsic state, sampler state, and what the
034   recorder makes observable.
035
      We examine identity-lock time on a blockwise-decoded LLaDA-style model (Nie et al., 2025; 2026).
036   An apparent period of 32 motivates a matched composite intervention over the block partition and
037   its required per-block step allocation: the model, 300 questions, canvas, total NFE, decoding rule,
038   and temperature remain ﬁxed. The active-block recorder, however, logs only masked positions in
039   the currently active block. Its lock-time proﬁle is therefore deﬁned relative to the sampling window
040   (Figure 1).

321   The central estimand is discrete period identity. On the observed samples, positive min–max sepa-
322   ration is a deterministic property of the recorded proﬁles; the bootstrap then probes the stability of
323   period selection to resampling decodes. The selected period is stable under both the per-arm boot-
      strap and the joint paired bootstrap that re-applies one shared question-index draw to all ﬁve arms

      (Section 2); the detection rule ﬁres in 0.88–1.0 of resamples across arms, with the L32 development
325   control the weakest at Pr[sep(32)>0]=0.8805. A robustiﬁed non-extremal rank-sum separation still
326   selects L on all ﬁve arms. This does not establish an arbitrarily small effect or a universal period:

      Foundational discrete-diffusion work develops structured denoising, reparameterization, density-
      ratio estimation, and simpliﬁed masked diffusion (Austin et al., 2021; Zheng et al., 2024; Lou et al.,
457
      2024; Shi et al., 2024). These studies concern the model or sampler trajectory rather than an active-
458
      block recorder perturbed at ﬁxed model and NFE.
459
460   The nearest schedule-axis methods include fully parallel and semi-autoregressive decoding (Gu
461   et al., 2018; Wang et al., 2018), conﬁdence-ordered mask-predict and LLaDA-style decoding
462
      (Ghazvininejad et al., 2019; Nie et al., 2025; 2026), and variable or reversible schedules (Li et al.,
      2025; Sun et al., 2026; Wang et al., 2026). Appendix Table 13 checks whether each work reports
463
      position-resolved commit/lock timing and varies block length. Wang et al.’s positionwise cross-
464
      entropy periodicity is the closest partial precedent; the setup differences do not support a ﬁeld-wide
465   absence claim. Our content analysis instead relates ﬁrst-step conﬁdence to later recorder lock order-
466   ing; it is not a new remasking policy.
467
468
      Measurement studies likewise show that probes inherit task-format confounds, prompts can under-
      retrieve stored knowledge, and analysis choices shape the reported representation (Sahoo et al.,
469
      2026; Jiang et al., 2020; Belinkov & Glass, 2019). Construct validity therefore requires matching an
470
      instrument to its supported target (Westreich & Greenland, 2013); preregistration separates planned
471   from outcome-contingent analysis but does not validate that target (Nosek et al., 2018). Our two-
472   layer record applies this principle without the causal attribution of mediation analyses (Stolfo et al.,
473   2023). Appendix Table 14 gives the observation-window and scale comparison, including the dis-
474   tinct monitoring example of Feng et al. (2024). The post-treatment amendment remains visible, the
475   min–max result is restricted to strictly-L-supported candidates, and the DFT control covers only the
476   tested sinusoidal residual family.

478   6   C ONCLUSION
479
480   Under the tested active-block recorder, the detected identity-lock period follows block length. De-
481   velopment controls, matched arms, non-nested probes, bootstrap stability, and negative evidence
482   support that recorder-level statement. They do not distinguish model-intrinsic state, sampler state,
483   and recorder observability. The transferable result is the reporting rule: state what is reachable from
484   set composition before data, then separately state what the measured magnitudes show. Calibrate
485   position-decomposed measurements to the schedule that recorded them, and do not infer a mecha-
      nism or cross-bed relation that the intervention did not measure.

      A.3   M EASUREMENT CANON
649
650   For blockwise decoding observed through an active-block recorder, a position-resolved analysis
651   should:
652
653         1. record block length, per-block steps, and generated-canvas coordinates;
654         2. calibrate within-block windows to the generating run rather than a ﬁxed model constant;
655         3. report reachability twice: a priori, what estimator algebra and boundary-set composition
656            imply; from events, what the measured magnitude ordering implies. In particular, B32 ⊂
657            B16 does not force sep(32) ≤ 0;
658
            4. perturb the schedule when attributing a position-resolved diagnostic;
659
660
            5. test content claims against a structure-preserving null and report its granularity; and
661         6. mark cross-bed relationships unmeasured until the corresponding intervention is run.
662
663
      This is a measurement canon, not a mechanism claim. The registered estimator returns P ∗ = L on
      the displayed strictly L-supported proﬁles: non-degenerate jumps occur on all L boundaries and no
664
      L-multiple’s exclusive-boundary residual outscores the L-boundary minimum. It is not a guarantee
665
      for arbitrary residual proﬁles, and identifying commit internals would require instrumentation of
666   sampler-state transfer.
