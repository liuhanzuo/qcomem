                                                A BSTRACT
013
014
              Multiple-choice accuracy for a diffusion language model is not determined by the
015
              model alone: the scorer chooses which option tokens are visible, when they are
016
              revealed, and whether revealed ground-truth tokens are fed back into later steps.
017           We audit that measurement construction on one bounded bed: LLaDA-8B-Instruct
018           with balanced four-choice GSM8K-MC.
019
              On a conﬁrmatory n=400 pool s_conf exceeds the non-injected mc_uniform
020           reference by +13.0 pp at the per-candidate nmc,i = 2Li total-NFE-fair equaliza-
021           tion point (b/c = 109/57, exact p = 6.6×10−5 ; four tested budgets, conservative
022           read +9.0 pp). This refutes the narrow account that the advantage is an artifact
023           of the reference receiving less compute, but establishes no budget-independent
024           law. On a disjoint fresh pool a ﬁxed left-to-right order whose schedule selection
025           reads no model output still beats s_conf; conﬁdence ordering is therefore nei-
026           ther necessary nor uniquely optimal on this answer format. Rank-agreement and
027           distractor-perturbation diagnostics support only a bed-local, sign-consistent non-
028           interchangeability reading; we do not claim dose-response, cross-bed transfer, or
029
              isolated causal attribution to feedback.

      1   I NTRODUCTION
033
      Masked diffusion language models assign a multiple-choice answer only after a scoring protocol
034
      turns option-level denoising losses into a ranking. That protocol is part of the measurement instru-
035
      ment. In particular, a scorer may reveal candidate tokens sequentially and feed the known candidate
036   token back into the context, or it may score a randomly masked subset without such feedback. Even
037   two feedback-based scorers can use different reveal schedules. Treating their accuracies as if they
038   were readings from the same ruler hides a change in what was measured.
039
      We audit this construction on one LLaDA-8B-Instruct × GSM8K-MC four-option bed: two histor-
040
      ical balanced pilot pools, a conﬁrmatory 400-question pool for the reference-arm contrast, and a
041
      disjoint fresh 819-question pool for reveal order. The study makes three pool-speciﬁc claims:
042
043        1. On the tested n=400 reference-budget curve, s_conf’s advantage over the non-injected
044           reference is positive and signiﬁcant at every budget and narrows as the reference receives
045           more compute (Table 3, Figure 2). The registered matched-L point remains primary. At
046           the per-candidate total-NFE-fair 2L endpoint, where both arms receive 2Li forwards, the
047           same bed retains the +13.0 pp headline gap (exact p = 4.8 × 10−5 and 6.6 × 10−5 ); the
048           highest-budget read remains the conservative one. This rules out a less-reference-compute-
049           only account on the tested curve, but not budget dependence off the curve. Because the
050           comparison is a complete protocol swap, it does not isolate feedback causally.
051        2. On the disjoint fresh pool, reveal order under the tested persisted schedule survives its k=1
052           multiplicity family. A ﬁxed schedule whose selection reads no model output nevertheless
053           beats s_conf on average (Table 6); the margin strengthens in the answer-pool construction
              stratum and reverses at answer position 0 (Appendix Table 8). Conﬁdence ordering is

      Bed and pairing. Each item pairs one GSM8K question with four candidate answers scored by
      LLaDA-8B-Instruct (Nie et al., 2025) with frozen weights. Choice sets were frozen but differ by

      pool, and the fresh pool is internally heterogeneous by construction: the historical, n = 400, and
126
      ﬁrst 336 fresh items use deterministic near-miss distractors; the remaining 483 fresh items use three
127   distinct values drawn from a frozen GSM8K answer pool. Every arm reuses the recorded choice set.
128   Historical and n = 400 pools are exactly answer-position balanced; the fresh pool is approximate.
129   Fresh-pool contrasts therefore mix protocol effects with construction stratum, and post-hoc position
130   standardization leaves the fresh–pilot difference, so we report them as strata-confounded. Inference
131   uses BF16, no temperature, and no generation. Conﬁdence and ﬁxed schedules are deterministic; the
132   seeded-random result is one persisted schedule. Because its runner used an unpinned Python string
133   hash, replay is anchored to stored per-candidate orders, not the nominal seed alone. Appendix H
134   lists identiﬁers, prompts, orders, and seeds. Historical pools use 12 or 16 items per answer position

      (n = 48/64); the n = 400 and fresh n = 819 pools are non-overlapping. Within each pool,

139

140
      the candidate is visible. It sums the per-step cross-entropies and selects the option with minimum
141   score. s_conf chooses the next minimum-cross-entropy position; rand_inject uses a deter-
142   ministically seeded random order; anti_inject chooses the maximum-cross-entropy position;

461   Bed, mechanism, and reliability. One checkpoint and one four-choice GSM8K bed support no
462   cross-model, task, or judge prediction. The s_conf–mc_uniform swap changes feedback, path,
463   and passes jointly, so the tested compute result does not factorize the mechanism. The frozen re-
464   run and leave-one-out audit pass their stated gates, but neither adds multi-seed evidence; multi-seed
465
      accuracy and τ require new decoding, and the 48-item self-τ remains a noise ﬂoor (Appendix Ta-
      ble 21).

      External validity and compute. The design supports no dose response, concavity, or cross-bed
468
      prediction band; the ﬁve-choice probe remains UNDETERMINED (Appendix Table 11), and length-
469
      normalized likelihood was not measured. The compute ledger covers scoring rather than generation.
470   Exact 2L rules out the narrow artifact only on the tested curve, while the historical high-budget
471   n = 64 row remains underpowered (Table 3; Appendix Table 20).

      8   C ONCLUSION
475
      On this single bed, the scoring protocol materially changes the measurement. Conﬁdence injec-
476
      tion beats the tested non-injected reference across the measured budget curve, including exact 2L,
477
      which rules out only a less-reference-compute account. A ﬁxed schedule wins on average on the
478   fresh pool but reverses at position 0 (Appendix Table 8), so conﬁdence ordering is neither nec-
479   essary nor uniquely optimal on this answer format. These ﬁndings establish bed-local protocol
480   non-interchangeability; off-curve, multi-seed, causal, and generalization claims remain unresolved
481   (Appendix H).
