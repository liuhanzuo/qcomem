009                                              A BSTRACT
010
011            A score recorded when a masked-diffusion decoder commits a token and the cor-
012            rectness of its final answer are measurements at different levels. We audit the
013            evidence and estimand behind their cross-level mean difference in two source-
014            reported records. One record gives a natural-strata difference of +0.2227; another,
015            on a different reported 400-item pool, gives a paired +37-token, fixed-NFE=64
016
               filler contrast of −0.0075. These point values concern different samples and es-
               timands, and no joint comparison is defined or tested. Their juxtaposition there-
017
               fore has no shared inferential interpretation. We independently check only the
018
               algebraic estimand definitions and arithmetic printed from the transcribed values.
019            The empirical measurements themselves are source-reported and cannot be in-
020            dependently verified because the current package lacks the item records, exact
021            intervention, decode traces, and analysis implementation. The paired estimand is
022            consequently a descriptive finite-pool quantity conditional on one recorded decode
023            realization, not expected decoder behavior. The commit score is also not verified
024            as a probability of final-answer correctness. No experiment was run as part of this
025            audit.

      1   I NTRODUCTION
028
029
      Prompt length can covary with task content, formatting, and difficulty. A natural length association
030   therefore does not identify the effect of a prompt modification. This distinction is especially im-
031   portant for masked-diffusion decoding, where prompt construction, the unmasking trajectory, and a
032   fixed inference budget can interact. We examine one source-described GSM8K (Cobbe et al., 2021)
033   / LLaDA-8B-Instruct (Nie et al., 2025) record that places two analyses side by side: an independent
034   natural-strata analysis and a paired fixed-filler analysis.
035
      The central quantity is a cross-level mean score–outcome difference: the mean of a decoder’s
036   recorded token-commit score minus mean final-answer correctness. The source artifacts call this
037   quantity SCE; we retain that string only as an internal artifact alias. We do not call the score con-
038   fidence or an answer probability because the package contains neither the token-to-answer aggre-
039   gation nor construct-validation evidence. The quantity remains arithmetically well defined as a
040   directional summary, but its zero point is not a calibration certificate (Ao et al., 2023; Seligmann
041   et al., 2023; Cruz et al., 2024).
042   This paper is an evidence and estimand audit. It asks what the source-described paired summary
043   estimates, which statements can be checked from the current package, and which empirical conclu-
044   sions remain unsupported. The natural-strata and fixed-filler point values are catalogued as distinct
045   source records; neither is used to decide what the other means. The paper contributes:
046
047        1. a finite-pool estimand conditional on the recorded single-decode realization;
048        2. a separation between independently checkable definitions and printed arithmetic, on the
049           one hand, and empirical measurements that can only be transcribed, on the other; and
050        3. a package inventory that makes the unavailable treatment, sample, score, decode, and im-
051           plementation evidence explicit.
052
053   Measurement status. Throughout, SR / CV means source-reported and not independently verifi-
      able from the current package. Every empirical numerical value, count, and dose in the manuscript

141   The source separately reports the paired-filler point −0.0075 and the natural-strata point +0.2227.
142   We do not compute a cross-record difference, ratio, or ordering from them. They concern different
143   item sets and estimands, and the package provides neither a common-sample functional nor a joint
144   analysis. Their juxtaposition is an index of two source records, not an empirical comparison.
145   The source-displayed score arm means have three decimal places, whereas the separately reported
146   score contrast has four. Without unrounded records, subtraction of the printed means need not
147   recover that contrast. This is unresolved rounding opacity, not evidence of a numerical inconsistency.
148   The current package supports only an audit of the formal target and evidence boundary; it cannot
149   establish intervention fidelity or explain the natural length association.

151   5     C LOSEST NEIGHBORING WORK AND BOUNDARY
152
153   Prior work establishes that prompt choices can change calibration-related behavior, but the verified
154   neighboring studies do not test this exact operation. Xia et al. (Xia et al., 2025) study calibration
155   across four prompt styles; their analysis does not isolate prompt length, content-free filler, masked-
156   diffusion commit scores, or a same-item +37-token intervention. Zhao et al. (Zhao et al., 2024)
157   study six prompting strategies and propose a confidence-calibration prompting method; the prompt-
158   ing strategy and elicited confidence change, rather than the source-described fixed filler under fixed
159   diffusion compute. Kamoda et al. (Kamoda et al., 2023) show that prompt variations affect factual-
160
      probing outputs and evaluate prompt augmentation as a calibration intervention; they use prompt
      variants and ensembling for factual probing, not the present fixed-NFE masked-diffusion filler esti-
161
      mand.

      For measurement context, signed mean score–label summaries and their cancellation limits predate

      6     L IMITATIONS , AVAILABILITY, AND DISCLOSURE
169
170   Scientific limits. The record covers one reported model–task setting, one fixed filler description,
171   one dose, one inference budget, and one stored decode per item–arm. The exact sample and inter-
172   vention are missing. The observational and paired analyses use different targets; the score construct
173   is not verified as an answer probability; and decoder-run variation is unavailable. These limits pre-
174   clude causal, decoder-average, cross-model, cross-task, or transport conclusions.
175
176   What the current package contains. The mutable authoring directory contains main.tex,
177   refs.bib, the ICLR style files, and compiled manuscript and auxiliary files. It contains no
178   figure asset, and this manuscript includes no figure. The directory does not contain experimen-
179
      tal records A32-E1 or A32-E2, literal prompts, item manifests, preregistration, protocol or analy-
      sis code, model/tokenizer revisions, decode configuration, an environment lock, or an evidence-ID
180
      crosswalk. No empirical value was rerun or independently recomputed.

      7     C ONCLUSION
188
189
      This audit formalizes a descriptive finite-pool, recorded-single-decode estimand for the source-
190   reported filler point −0.0075. A separate natural-strata record reports +0.2227. The samples and
191   estimands differ, and no joint analysis is available, so the two point values support no cross-record
192   inference. The estimand definitions and printed decomposition arithmetic can be checked inde-
193   pendently; the empirical inputs can only be transcribed. Missing records, intervention and sample
194   construction, score semantics, and decoder-replay evidence prevent broader interpretation.
