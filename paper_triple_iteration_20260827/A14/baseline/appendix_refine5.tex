For restart fraction $m$, designated-candidate indicator $\iota_i$, and
$n_c(q)$ candidates in question $q$, the closed form is
\begin{align*}
\mathrm{NFE}_i(m)&=\min(6/L_i,2)\,(1-m_L^*)^{-2}m,
&m_L^*&=(1-2^{-1/L_i})^{-1},\\
\widehat\Delta_q(m)&=n_c(q)^{-1}\sum_{i\in q}\iota_i(1-m)^{L_i}.&&
\end{align*}
The reported mean spans $Q{=}819$ questions and its interval uses $R{=}2000$
question bootstraps (seed $20260821$) \citep{efron1994bootstrap}; the zero row
is an arm-independent identity, and the nonzero rows are length-model
projections rather than evidence about an accuracy effect.
