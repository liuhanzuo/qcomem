# A14 assumptions and semantic lock

- The frozen baseline PDF and TeX are the authoritative author-provided record.
- All reported numbers are preserved unless a located artifact supports correction.
- `source_reported/cannot_verify_locally` means the value appears in the frozen
  manuscript but its referenced raw result was not found during this run.
- The study is one LLaDA-8B-Instruct by balanced four-choice GSM8K-MC bed.
- Exact forward-count matching is not equivalent to measured wall-clock,
  FLOP, memory, or energy matching.
- The complete protocol swap does not isolate feedback, reveal order, path,
  selection, or estimator as an individual causal component.
- Every empirical estimand is a contrast on a named finite pool conditional on
  the source manuscript's recorded scorer realization. It is not an expectation
  over item sampling, random-mask streams, or reveal schedules.
- No timestamped registration, complete decision chronology, or complete
  defined-arm outcome matrix is available. Every displayed analysis is
  exploratory and descriptive; matched-$L$ is foregrounded only as the nominal
  scoring-draw-matched comparison. The $2L/2L$ point remains post-selected, and
  128 draws is one displayed higher-budget point.
- The source defines five protocols, but disclosed outcomes cover only s_conf,
  mc_uniform, rand_inject, and fixed_inject; anti_inject has no reported outcome
  in the current package.
- Effects, accuracies, and discordant counts may be reported descriptively.
  Confidence intervals, p-values, significance language, and cross-pool test
  interpretations are excluded because the fixed-pool realized-trace target
  supplies no probability model.
- The fixed-schedule result is one bed-local, construction-dependent comparison;
  it is not a theorem about confidence-guided scoring.
- All empirical measurements must be labelled
  `source_reported/cannot_verify_from_current_package`; no missing artifact was
  rerun or reconstructed in the current-evidence revision.
- No cross-model, cross-task, dose-response, compute-efficiency, or universal
  mechanism claim may be introduced without new evidence.
