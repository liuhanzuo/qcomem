# A31 assumptions and semantic lock

- The frozen baseline PDF and TeX are authoritative for reported values.
- The active-block recorder defines the observation window; identity-lock time
  is not treated as a direct model-mechanism measurement.
- Block partition and required per-block step allocation co-vary in the available
  intervention and cannot be causally separated.
- The headline empirical result is restricted to the source-reported complete
  rows for two main arms and one reused L32 development control. Their bootstrap
  is a sample-stability diagnostic, not calibrated false-positive control,
  coverage, or proof that an off-grid component is absent.
- The L24/L48 non-nested arms are low-boundary probes; L48 has one candidate
  boundary and cannot support a broad period-law claim.
- The L32 arm is a frozen development control, not an independent confirmation.
- The evidence tiers are one reused L32 development control, two main arms
  (L16/L64), and two sequential probes (L24/L48); aggregate wording must preserve
  these roles. The sequential probes are unauditable side observations, not
  headline confirmations or verified argmaxes.
- Integer lock times do not imply integer medians for even samples. The reported
  half-integral values are compatible with a conventional midpoint median, but
  the exact implementation is unavailable and remains unresolved.
- The finite difference domain is `r in {0,...,C-2}`; candidates are admissible
  only when their boundary and interior sets on that domain are both nonempty.
- The available package does not determine whether recorder step labels are
  global, block-local, or per-position ordinals, nor how missing, censored, or
  terminal cases entered the reported medians. Do not choose or imply a rule.
- The rank-sum readout is an alternative statistic on the same profiles, not
  independent calibrated inference. Its complete candidate vectors and
  implementation are absent.
- The source-reported `10/25` original-gate aggregate cannot be mapped to the
  displayed four-candidate canvas-256 grids. The exact audit grid is unlocated;
  do not infer per-arm candidate lists or denominators.
- The paper identity is a recorder-relative evidence audit of a narrow case
  study. Use direct prose for the audited design, but never imply new execution,
  ownership of unavailable artifacts, causal attribution, or generalization.
- Every empirical number is source-reported/cannot-verify-locally; no analysis
  rerun may be claimed.
- No model-mechanism, cross-model, cross-schedule, cross-recorder, or universal
  claim may be introduced.
